## What Was Processed

### Doc 1 — Agent Authorization: Union of Everything Possible
**Type:** Arch brief | **File:** `briefs/07/02/authorization-and-maturity-model/v0.33.40__arch-brief__sg-send-agent-authorization-union-of-possible-expected-unexpected-delta-blast-radius-hope-driven.md`

An agent's real authorization is the transitive closure of everything reachable from the grant, not the words of the grant. The key quantity is the delta between expected and unexpected permissions. Two awareness gaps: the granter doesn't know what it's actually granting; the original delegator never consented to re-delegation. The concrete examples (inbox → all resettable accounts; desktop → all stored credentials + sessions; code execution → potential admin escalation) make this visceral. The "hope-driven development" label names the anti-pattern: hoping the agent behaves and finds no more, while you remain accountable. The control is a scoped capability certificate with a block list.

**PROPOSED items introduced (6):** AuthorizationClosure entity, CapabilityCertificate with block list, MomentOfAuthorization with re-delegation consent field, expected-vs-unexpected delta surfacing, permission-amplifying artifact classification, de-authorization over the closure.

### Doc 2 — RAMM: Risk Acceptance Maturity Model
**Type:** Arch brief (semantic graph) | **File:** `briefs/07/02/authorization-and-maturity-model/v0.33.40__arch-brief__sg-send-risk-acceptance-maturity-model-ramm-graph-native-levels-agentic-crosswalk.md`

A graph-native Risk Acceptance Maturity Model with five levels expressed as Node Type Formulas (testable graph predicates). Maturity is computed, not claimed. Ten entity classes assembling what the corpus already has into a formal schema. Bridges to OWASP Risk Rating Methodology, SAMM, RIMS, and public-sector maturity models via the ontologies-of-ontologies model. Agentic RAMM overlay adds CapabilityCertificate, AuthorizationClosure, MomentOfAuthorization, and AgentTwin. OWASP Incubator project submission is called out as the immediate next action.

**PROPOSED items introduced (10):** RAMM as a published standard, 10 RAMM entity classes, 5 maturity level predicates, example maturity queries, Agentic RAMM overlay (4 entities), OWASP/RIMS bridges, RAMM published site + public repo, OWASP Incubator submission.

### Doc 3 — Accountability Paradox of Gen AI
**Type:** Strategy brief | **File:** `briefs/07/02/root-cause-and-accountability/v0.33.40__strategy-brief__accountability-paradox-gen-ai-black-box-delegation-vs-scaling-evidence-decision-trees-semantic-graphs.md`

Two forces: Force One delegates decisions to black boxes, producing fuzzy accountability and approval fatigue. Force Two uses LLMs to scale control flows, decision trees, and evidence, so mission-critical decisions return to explicit, traceable logic. "Use LLMs not to use LLMs" — the model builds the scaffolding; deterministic graph predicates make the decision. Semantic knowledge graphs are the structure that makes this legible at scale. Friction: visibility exposes inefficiency and resists those who benefit from unaccountability.

**PROPOSED items introduced (5):** accountability-in-the-loop detection pattern, approval-fatigue metric, LLM-confidence bounding pattern, scaled accountability architecture, decision-graph population by LLM.
