## Executive Summary

22 unprocessed human briefs — from 05/30, 05/31, and 06/01 — were catalogued and processed this session. These briefs were committed to the repo after the 06/01 librarian session ran (which found no human briefs that day).

The 22 briefs represent **a major strategic pivot** toward SG/Vault as the substrate for an agentic partner ecosystem and skills creator economy. This is not an extension of the existing roadmap — it is a new top-level track that must be sequenced against Vault Chat, connectors, and the assessment series.

**Four themes:**

1. **Artefact layer (05/30):** Vault-per-standard creates semantic graph artefacts from compliance documents (P-281). Public-preview-with-read-only-key makes them shareable in one click (P-282). The vulnerability threat-intel vault is the first demo (P-283). All three are unblocked or nearly so.

2. **Content + publishing engine (05/30–05/31):** The website becomes a living knowledge base controlled by agents — per-section vaults, a vault CI pipeline, industry-specific pages with embedded vault examples, and every page serving a semantic graph for LLMs/crawlers via Lambda@Edge (P-284–P-287). The CI pipeline (P-285) is blocked on P-279.

3. **Partner ecosystem (06/01):** Seven partners map onto a layered stack: Netlify (hosting), Daytona (execution), Convex (live backend), Pi (harness interop), HeyGen (video), Skills Graph (foundation), Tavon (channel). Each has a clean integration boundary with the vault. Netlify is the recommended first prototype — lowest effort, mostly wiring existing pieces.

4. **Skills creator economy (06/01):** Base vaults (P-295), trustworthy marketplace scoring (P-296), opinionated expertise as the commercial model (P-297), comparison + CLI-wrapping (P-298), OpenRouter billing broker (P-299). The `use-sg-playwright/SKILL.md` committed in v0.31.16 is the first real base vault candidate.
