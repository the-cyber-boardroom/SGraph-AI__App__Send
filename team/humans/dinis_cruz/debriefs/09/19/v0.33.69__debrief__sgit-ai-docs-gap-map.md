# Debrief — sgit.ai documentation vs the shipped vault platform (2026-09-19)

## Executive summary

You asked for a review of the current vault + vault-app features compared with the guidance at sgit.ai/llms.txt, mapped into a brief for the team that maintains that site. Done, code-verified on both sides: the site's source (`SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website` v0.2.82, commit `8dad6960`, cloned — the sandbox cannot reach sgit.ai over the network) against this repo's `dev` at v0.33.69.

**Verdict.** The site is honest and well-built but describes the platform as of mid-August. It has no mention of the two largest things shipped since — declared mounts (with receipts and the sync discipline) and the read-key open forms — and **seven statements on it would break an agent that follows them**, two of them copy-paste examples that silently no-op (camelCase `sg.append` arguments; `host_events` as an array). Full detail, per page and with file:line, in the brief.
