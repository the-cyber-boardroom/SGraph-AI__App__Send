## Executive Summary

Day 65 (15 May) adds 10 documents that are research-heavy and strategy-heavy: four research
briefs (Firecracker capabilities, Firecracker vs Docker/Podman performance, Nitro Enclaves,
AgentCore capabilities and pricing), four strategy briefs (Firecracker direct use decision,
AgentCore layering, Nitro Enclaves in the ZK ecosystem, USDC and micropayments), one addendum
(S3 and observability urgency reframe), and the day's synthesis in the daily brief.

Three things stand out from this batch:

**1. The Firecracker answer changed.** February 2026 nested-virt on C8i/M8i/R8i dropped the
entry cost from $3+/hr to ~$0.089/hr (30× cheaper). The strategic posture shifts from "watch
and wait" to "prototype selectively now." Three specific use cases justify the engineering
investment: AI agent code execution sandboxes (isolation requirement), snapshot-fast Playwright
fleets, and vault-attached compute with snapshots.

**2. Nitro Enclaves add a fourth ZK boundary.** The current zero-knowledge contract says
"we cannot see your plaintext." Nitro Enclaves extend this: "we cannot see your plaintext,
and when server-side processing is necessary, it happens inside an attested enclave running
code you can verify." This opens seven new product surfaces (server-side search, vault AI
inference, multi-party computation, verifiable operations, server-side signing, confidential
credential manager, async vault sharing).

**3. AgentCore reduces the build list.** Several previously-planned builds (sandboxed code
execution, cloud browser, in-session memory, OAuth, voice streaming) now flip to
consume-not-build. AgentCore is not a competitor — it is a substrate with SG layering on top.
Six resell product candidates named. The freed engineering time goes into the distinctive
layers (vault grounding, credential manager, manager vaults).

**32 new PROPOSED items** added to the reality document. **0 new EXISTS items** (no code shipped).
