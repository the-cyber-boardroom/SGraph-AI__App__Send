## What This Is

Two infographics visualising the comparison between our serverless per-tenant VM model and the Kubernetes multi-tenant model. Both were generated by external AI systems (Perplexity and OpenAI / ChatGPT image generation) from the underlying research, and both are included in the daily pack as visual aids alongside the research brief and the strategic analysis.

This brief is a reference index: what each infographic shows, what each does well, what differs between them, and how to use them.

## What The Perplexity Version Does Well

The Perplexity infographic uses a **strict side-by-side comparison** layout: our model in blue on the left, Kubernetes in green on the right. Six numbered dimensions stacked vertically, each with a small icon and a one-line description.

Strengths:

- **Scannable.** A reader gets the comparison in 30 seconds.
- **Visual symmetry.** The strict left-right balance makes the differences pop.
- **Colour coding is intuitive.** Blue for our model, green for Kubernetes. No legend needed.
- **Dense but not overwhelming.** Six rows is roughly the right number for a single screen.

Best used for: a quick reference, a slide in a partner deck, a Twitter / LinkedIn share. When you need to show the comparison fast.

## What The OpenAI Version Does Well

The OpenAI infographic uses a **top-down narrative** layout: starts with an overview pair, drops into a comparison table for the structural points, then expands into thematic cards (operational complexity, resilience & failure modes, performance & scaling, security & isolation, cost model), and closes with a best-fit summary and an assessment block.

Strengths:

- **More information density.** It captures more of the research than the side-by-side version.
- **Best-fit summary panel.** Direct "use this when... use that when..." guidance with checkmarks. Practical.
- **Cost model balance.** The literal scales visual for cost comparison is intuitive.
- **Assessment paragraph at the bottom.** Captures the strategic framing in narrative form.

Best used for: a longer-form share, a document inclusion, a page on sgraph.ai, a leave-behind after a meeting. When the reader has more than 30 seconds and you want to communicate the nuance.

## How They Differ

| Dimension | Perplexity Version | OpenAI Version |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|
| Layout direction | Side-by-side | Top-down |
| Reading time | ~30 seconds | ~2-3 minutes |
| Best-fit guidance | Implicit | Explicit panel |
| Cost visualisation | Bar chart at zero vs constant bars | Scale balance metaphor |
| Assessment framing | Not present | Explicit paragraph |
| Visual density | Lower | Higher |
| Tone | Reference card | Position paper visual |

Neither is "better." They serve different reading contexts.
