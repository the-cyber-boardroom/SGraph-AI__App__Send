## What This Is

A first pass at the series of Wardley maps specified in the maps brief, drawn text-first: **eight maps, each anchored at a user need and positioned along the evolution axis from genesis to commodity, each captured as the reality today and as the shift our service creates, covering the exec who wants to use AI safely, the person who needs to understand and propagate risk, the person who needs to delegate mandates without liability, the agent as a user, the security team, the financial team, the vendor of an agentic solution, and the competitive landscape; the picture that recurs is that the components a user assumes are mature, safe AI use, risk understanding, authorization, are genesis or custom-built today, while execution is already a commodity, and our service moves the risk, data, mandate, and acceptance components rightward toward product without pretending to move the things we do not own, in-line enforcement, the safe uninjected decision, and legal liability, which stay where they are.** It is the companion to the maps brief (cross-ref: the v0.33.33 Wardley-maps brief and primer, the v0.33.32 agent-mandate-graph and observability briefs, the v0.33.40 risk-acceptance brief, and the v0.33.31 mandate-is-delegation brief). New contributions: **the eight drawn maps, the today-versus-with-service positions, and the cross-cutting reading.**

## How To Read These Maps

Each map is anchored at a user need at the top, the most visible thing. Below it sit the components it depends on, less visible as you go down. Every component is placed on the evolution axis: **genesis** (novel, uncertain), **custom-built** (made by hand for the case), **product** (repeatable, bought), and **commodity** (standardised, taken for granted). For each component the position table gives its visibility, its position today, and its position with our service, so the before and after is the shift between the last two columns. The two flagship maps also have an ASCII sketch of today's reality. These positions are a first pass, meant to be challenged, and the maps are authored text-first so they can live in the agent mandate vault graph, where a map query is a graph query.

A note that holds across all eight: our service moves components toward product by supplying data, risk understanding, the mandate model, and the acceptance flow, out of band. It does not move what we do not own, so in-line enforcement stays custom, the safe uninjected decision stays genesis, and legal liability stays with counsel. Saying so is what keeps the maps honest.

## Map 1: The Exec Or Stakeholder, "Use AI Safely"

The exec wants to promote and use AI within the risk appetite. Safe is not secure: safe means within the threshold of risk the business will accept, which needs good data, an acceptance, and the ability to enforce.

```
 HIGH   | Use AI safely  (the need; a custom, bespoke effort today)
 vis    |
        | risk-acceptance-flow       risk-understanding
        | good-data-&-facts          the-mandate(authz)
        |                            ability-to-enforce
        |
 LOW    |                                               the-LLM    execution
        |                                               (model)    APIs/MCP
        +-------------------------------------------------------------------
           GENESIS          CUSTOM-BUILT         PRODUCT          COMMODITY
```

| Component | Visibility | Today | With our service |
|-----------|------------|-------|------------------|
| Use AI safely (the need) | High | Custom-built | Product |
| Risk acceptance flow | High | Genesis | Product |
| Risk understanding | Medium | Custom-built | Product |
| Good data and facts | Medium | Genesis | Product |
| The mandate (authorization) | Medium | Custom-built | Product |
| Ability to enforce | Low | Custom-built | Custom-built (out of band) |
| The LLM model | Low | Commodity | Commodity |
| Execution (APIs, MCP) | Low | Product | Commodity |

The reading: everything the exec assumes is a product, safe AI use, risk understanding, the mandate, is genesis or custom today; our service moves them rightward, but enforcement stays custom because we are out of band.

## Map 2: Understanding And Propagating Risk

The need is to understand the risk faced and to let acceptance propagate up and down the organisation. Today there is no good way to do this, and cost belongs on the same map because cost correlates with what is actually happening.

| Component | Visibility | Today | With our service |
|-----------|------------|-------|------------------|
| Risk acceptance flow (up and down) | High | Genesis | Product |
| Risk understanding | High | Custom-built | Product |
| The risk graph (blast radius, paths) | Medium | Genesis | Product |
| Evidence and good data | Medium | Custom-built | Product |
| Cost mapping (cost as a risk dimension) | Medium | Genesis | Product |
| Propagation and sign-off mechanism | Low | Custom-built | Product |

The reading: the entire flow is genesis or custom today; the service productises the graph, the evidence, the cost dimension, and the sign-off path.

## Map 3: Delegation Of Mandates

The need is to delegate a mandate in a way that is safe and does not leave the delegator carrying liability.

| Component | Visibility | Today | With our service |
|-----------|------------|-------|------------------|
| Safe delegation (the need) | High | Genesis | Product |
| The right to delegate (authority) | High | Genesis | Custom-built to Product |
| The mandate model | Medium | Custom-built | Product |
| Liability and consent handling | Medium | Genesis | Genesis (defer to counsel) |
| Sign-off mechanism | Low | Custom-built | Product |

The reading: the safe, liability-free delegation path is largely genesis; we can productise the mandate model and the sign-off, but the legal liability piece stays genesis and belongs with counsel.

## Map 4: The Agent As A User

The agent is a user too. It needs to ask for, receive, and check permissions, make decisions, validate controls, and execute. This is the sharpest map, because execution is a commodity while the safe, uninjected decision is genesis.

```
 HIGH   | Act (do the task)   (the agent's need)
 vis    |
        | make-safe-decision         ask/check-permissions
        | (uninjected, on-task)      validate-&-exchange-controls
        |
        |                                               the-LLM    execution
 LOW    |                                               (model)    APIs/MCP/machines
        +-------------------------------------------------------------------
           GENESIS          CUSTOM-BUILT         PRODUCT          COMMODITY
```

| Component | Visibility | Today | With our service |
|-----------|------------|-------|------------------|
| Act (do the task) | High | Custom-built | Product |
| Ask for and check permissions | High | Custom-built | Product |
| Make a safe, uninjected, on-task decision | Medium | Genesis | Genesis (the hard part) |
| Validate and exchange controls | Medium | Custom-built | Product |
| Execute commands | Low | Product | Commodity |
| Execution substrate (APIs, MCP, machines) | Low | Commodity | Commodity |
| The LLM model | Low | Commodity | Commodity |

The reading: the mature execution layer sits far right, but the safe decision, whether the LLM can be trusted to act without being injected or going off task, sits in genesis. We can productise the permission and control layers and supply the mandate and the visibility, but the safe decision stays genesis, which is exactly why we operate out of band rather than trusting it in line.

## Map 5: The Security Team

The need is to reduce the risk of agents and to integrate with the tools the team already runs.

| Component | Visibility | Today | With our service |
|-----------|------------|-------|------------------|
| Reduce agent risk (the need) | High | Custom-built | Product |
| Detection and visibility of agent action | High | Genesis | Product |
| The risk and blast-radius map | Medium | Genesis | Product |
| Incident handling (redefined around expiry) | Medium | Custom-built | Product |
| SIEM and existing-tool integration | Low | Commodity | Commodity (we feed it) |

The reading: detection of agent action and the risk map are genesis today; we productise them and feed the commodity SIEM rather than replacing it, which is the live-off-the-land move.

## Map 6: The Financial Team

The need is to understand and control the cost and spend of agentic AI, the blank-check problem.

| Component | Visibility | Today | With our service |
|-----------|------------|-------|------------------|
| Control agentic cost (the need) | High | Genesis | Product |
| Cost visibility per team and agent | High | Genesis | Product |
| Cost as a risk dimension | Medium | Genesis | Product |
| The spend mandate (cap and accountability) | Medium | Custom-built | Product |
| Provider metering and billing | Low | Product | Commodity |

The reading: the bill is a product, but visibility and control are genesis, which is how a sign-off becomes a blank check; the service makes spend a visible risk dimension with accountability.

## Map 7: The Vendor Of An Agentic Solution

Every company that puts an LLM in line must understand its own capabilities and present a credible risk mapping to its own customers.

| Component | Visibility | Today | With our service |
|-----------|------------|-------|------------------|
| Present credible risk to customers (the need) | High | Genesis | Product |
| Understand own capabilities and blast radius | High | Custom-built | Product |
| The risk-mapping artifact | Medium | Genesis | Product |
| Reverse-engineerable posture (burden-shift) | Medium | Genesis | Custom-built to Product |
| The in-line LLM integration | Low | Custom-built | Product |

The reading: the ability to show customers a credible risk mapping does not exist as a product today; this is where the burden-shift lands, and the service gives the vendor the artifact to present.

|-----------------|----------------|------------------|
| Mandate, blast radius, risk acceptance (us) | Genesis | The open lane we create |

The reading: the mandate lane is genesis and open, while everything adjacent, identity, JIT, observability, SIEM, the models, is product or commodity to live off the land.

## Cross-Cutting Reading

- **The commodity illusion.** Across the maps, the components a user assumes are mature, safe AI use, risk understanding, authorization, the safe decision, sit in genesis or custom, while execution and the model are already commodities. The gap between the assumption and the reality is the case for the service.
- **What we move, and what we do not.** Our service moves risk, data, mandate, acceptance, detection, and cost rightward toward product. It does not move in-line enforcement, the safe uninjected decision, or legal liability, which honestly stay where they are.
- **The gameplays the maps imply.** Ecosystem play and living off the land, feed the commodity SIEM and JIT players rather than rebuild them; innovate-leverage-commoditise on the genesis mandate lane; and a tower-and-moat position around the mandate graph as the thing others come to depend on.
- **Doctrine.** The maps follow start with user needs, manage inertia, the pull toward the in-line hope model, and challenge assumptions, the assumption that authorization is a product.

## Honest Notes

- **Placements are a first pass.** Each position is a judgement to be challenged with evidence and the risk-dimensions work.
- **Some things stay immature even with us.** Enforcement, the safe decision, and liability are honestly left where they are; the maps do not pretend otherwise.
- **The landscape map is a snapshot.** Player positions move; treat it as of today and revisit.
- **Visibility ordering is approximate.** The vertical placement is indicative, not exact.
