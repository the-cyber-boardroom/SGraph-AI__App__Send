## Through-Lines

- **Risk, owned.** Surface it, accept it for an interval, underwrite it; there is no deny button.
- **The lethal trifecta.** Reads untrusted content, reaches the internet, can act on its own, the personal scenario and the Calendly review both turn on it.
- **Sovereignty from the keys, not the data.** Client-side encryption, and only the endpoints ever see the clear text.
- **Show, do not tell.** Medical analogies and infographics make the pattern land for a non-technical audience.
- **Self-contained.** A worked review and a debrief that each stand alone.
