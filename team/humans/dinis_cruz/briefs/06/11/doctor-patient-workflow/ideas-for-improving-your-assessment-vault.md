# Ideas For Improving Your Assessment Vault

**A menu of ideas, and a map of what is possible.** These are suggestions for changes you can make to your own vault. Pick the ones you like. You (or your Claude session, using the cloning guide) can implement them. Some need support on our side, which is noted. You do not have to do any of this, and you do not have to do it in order.

A useful thing to know up front: your vault can do more than it might look like. This document doubles as a list of what the vault is capable of, so you and your Claude session know what is on the table.

---

## Start Here: Quick Wins

These are small, high-value, and easy to add first.

**A friendly beta notice.** Show a short, friendly note (not a scary red warning) that says something like: "This is a beta. Please do not enter any confidential information, just try it out." This sets the right expectations and keeps early testers comfortable.

**Ask for feedback.** Add a simple way to give feedback, on each page or at least at the end. What we most want to learn is how the experience felt, whether the flow made sense, and whether the questions felt right. This is the whole point of the early version: to learn and improve.

**Let people comment on a question.** When someone is answering, let them flag a question as "not relevant to me" or "not quite the right question." That feedback is gold for improving the assessment.

## The Chat

The chat is one of the most useful parts, and it can be much clearer.

**Make the chat always visible.** Right now it is easy to miss because you have to click to find it. A persistent "Chat here, ask questions" area works much better.

**Separate the two kinds of conversation.** There is the conversation about the assessment itself, and there is general chat with us. Keep them as two clearly distinct things so they do not get tangled.

**Set expectations: it is not live.** Add a gentle note that there is no bot and no person waiting on the other side right now, so replies come when one of us, or you, or an admin is online.

**Allow screenshots.** Let someone paste a screenshot into the chat. When something looks wrong, a picture is the fastest way to tell us what happened.

**Keep it tidy.** Make sure each chat is dated and clearly linked to the right assessment, so a conversation always has its context.

## The Look And Feel (Three Building Blocks)

Your vault's appearance can be controlled with three independent building blocks. You can expose these to people, or keep some behind a settings panel.

**1. Theme.** Fonts and colours, light and dark, high contrast. The visual style.

**2. Layout.** Where things are placed on the screen. The same content can be arranged in different ways, which lets you suit different people and cultures.

**3. Language.** Not just a language, but a language-and-region pairing, for example Portuguese (Portugal) versus Portuguese (Brazil), or English (UK) versus English (US). You can build up a matrix of these over time.

To make the last two work well, it helps to move the words out of the page itself and into separate, easy-to-translate files, and to keep the interface wording separate from the assessment questions, so each can be looked after on its own.

## Smarter Results

The results screen can do much more than show a number.

**Let people change their answers and see what happens.** Allowing someone to revisit and modify a response, and see how their answers and their result shift, is genuinely engaging.

**Explain the score.** Show more about how the score is worked out, not just the final figure.

**Show each answer's effect.** Let people see what each individual answer contributed to the score. It turns a black box into something they understand.

## Things That Need Our Side Too

A few ideas need support from our infrastructure, not just changes in your vault. Flag these to us and we will line them up.

**Feature switches.** We can give your vault the ability to turn features on and off, so a change can be in place but only switched on when it is ready, without disturbing anything that already works.

**Switches we control centrally.** We can also flip some of those switches, and even adjust the theme, from our side, so improvements can roll out without you having to do anything.

**Features that depend on our version.** Some features only work when our side is new enough to support them. We can make a feature wait quietly until that is true, so nothing ever appears half-working.

**Coming back to a saved vault.** Today a vault lives in the browser tab you started it in. Returning to it later (a bookmark or a saved link) is something we are still working out, so treat each session as self-contained for now.

## How To Use This Document

Pick the ideas you like. For the simple ones (the beta notice, feedback, the chat improvements), you can make the changes yourself using the cloning guide, or hand both documents to your Claude session and say "Read the cloning guide, clone my vault, and make these changes." For anything in the "needs our side too" section, let us know and we will support it. Start small, try one or two, and tell us how it goes, that feedback is exactly what we are looking for.
