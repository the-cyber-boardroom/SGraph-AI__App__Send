## The Through-Line

The three themes are one argument. The use cases say risk acceptance is understood only when it is experienced, and must be grounded in real incidents. The mini-sites work is how those use cases ship cheaply and safely, with a static, client-decrypted prod air-gapped from an editable QA. And the ontology thread is the engine underneath: what a node is should be computed by an explicit formula, grounded down to a measure, a twin, and reality, and classified up to a top business risk, with multiple owned formulas bridged rather than merged. The OAuth and 2:17am use cases are the ontology's first real inputs, and the external S/f/F/C bridge is the first proof that the architecture holds open to other people's definitions.
