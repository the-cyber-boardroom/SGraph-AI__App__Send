## What This Is

The approach, consolidated after a successful partner meeting where we aligned on the principles and the pain points: **the premise is that by presenting senior executives with the real risks they are buying and the real side-effects of what they have, fact-driven and graph-driven, the business will drive itself forward and, crucially, fund the actions to fix it, so the product drives behaviour change, making the organisation aware so it fixes itself; the core principles are risk management first, out of band, open source and no lock-in, living off the land, and creating the environment for awareness that drives good decisions and good behaviours and so reduces risk; the next step is to recreate the five partner deliverables, the four artifacts and the product-requirements document, aligned to this, focusing on what we add value to, and then to brainstorm the services, the player experiences, and the graph and ontology to visualise.** It is the approach for review, before the use cases (cross-ref: the v0.33.32 execution-memo-review, partner-artifacts-alignment, how-not-why, and observability briefs, the v0.33.40 blast-radius, risk-acceptance, and open-source-strategy briefs, and the go-to-market brief). New contributions: **the behaviour-change premise, the consolidated core principles, and the two-step plan.**

Where this comes from: **a good meeting, and an aligned direction.**

The project lead: **"the good news is I had a good meeting with the partner, and we aligned on the key principles and the key pain points: the focus on explaining the how, what privileges they have, the risk mapping, and the risk profile of what is possible. What I want is to create a document with the approach, so I can review it, and then we can look at specific use cases."** So this is the consolidation the partner agreed to, ahead of the use cases.

## The Premise: Drive Behaviour Change Through Risk Awareness

The core idea: **make them aware, and they will fund the fix.**

The project lead: **"if we present the risks to senior execs in a way that is fact-driven and graph-driven, with powerful risk analysis, the business will drive forward, and more importantly they will fund the actions to do something about it. A key premise is that we are driving behaviour change, driving the organisation to fix itself, by making it aware of the real risks it is buying and the real side-effects of what it has."** So the product's job is not to enforce; it is to make the real risk visible, fact-driven and graph-driven, so the organisation acts on it and pays to fix it. Awareness is the lever, and the funding follows the awareness.

## Why This Works

The mechanism: **a risk an exec can see is a risk an exec will fund.**

A fact-driven, graph-driven picture of the risk an organisation is buying is something an executive can understand, accept, and act on, in a way that a control they have to trust is not. Showing the real blast radius and the real side-effects creates the awareness that drives the decision and the funding, and the organisation fixes itself. This is restrict-to-unleash and the self-fulfilling visibility loop applied to behaviour change: we do not fix the organisation; we make it see, so it fixes itself (cross-ref: the blast-radius and partner-artifacts-alignment briefs).

## The Core Principles

The principles we aligned on, consolidated:

1. **Risk management first.** The first principle is driving risk-management decisions; everything serves that.
2. **Out of band.** Not in line; we provide visibility and a window to act, not enforcement (cross-ref: the execution-memo-review brief).
3. **Open source, no lock-in.** The trust and adoption model; the engine is open, and customers are never locked in (cross-ref: the open-source-strategy brief).
4. **Living off the land.** Leverage the energy and money already in the market, and make the existing tools more effective by connecting the dots and supplying the data they lack.
5. **Awareness that drives good decisions and behaviours.** Create the environment so the business sees what is going on, decides well, and behaves well.
6. **Reduce the risk.** The outcome: improve security and reduce the risk of what the organisation is doing.

The premise sits above the principles: the principles are how we drive the behaviour change.

## Honest Tensions

**Tension 1: awareness depends on the organisation acting.** We make them see; they must still act. Mitigation: the funding loop is the mechanism, a risk an exec can see is one they will fund.

**Tension 2: living off the land shares the value.** Partners hold part of the prize. Mitigation: we hold the data they lack and connect the dots, which is durable and avoids the single-point-of-failure risk.

**Tension 3: open source versus monetisation.** Free engine, paid operation. Mitigation: the open-core model, you pay to operate it safely at scale.
