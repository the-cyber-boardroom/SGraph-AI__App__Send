## Through-Lines

- **Fact, not hope.** Operate on what is possible and what was granted, not on the hope the agent behaves.
- **Out of band, not in line.** Provide visibility and a window to act; do not become the in-line single point of failure.
- **How and who, not why.** Explain how an action was possible and who authorised it; the why is hard and largely not ours.
- **Blast radius and risk acceptance.** The authorised universe a grant opens, and who accepts the risk it carries.
- **Awareness funds the fix.** Make the real, fact-driven, graph-driven risk visible, and the business drives and funds its own remediation.
- **Evidence, honestly.** Near-unquestionable public evidence, with confidence scores and honest gaps.
