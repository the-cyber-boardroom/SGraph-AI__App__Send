## What This Is

A research brief on Tavon (tavon.ai): what it is, how SG/Send could use and integrate with it, and how it could help businesses be created around it. It is one of a set of partner-integration briefs aimed at producing library pages and example vaults.

The short version: **Tavon is an agentic-AI implementation firm, strategy, building and shipping agent systems and integrations, and team enablement, built on the coding-agent breakthrough.** Unlike the other partners in this set (which are technical primitives), Tavon is a different kind of partner: a **channel and implementation partner** who takes businesses from AI interest to working automation. SG/Vault could be part of what such a firm deploys, and Tavon-style firms are exactly who could build businesses on SG/Vault for their clients.

## What Tavon Is

The research picture, drawn from Tavon's own site, current as of this brief:

- Tavon helps organisations "put AI agents to work," moving from curiosity to implementation with systems that deliver measurable results. Its positioning is that most "AI agents" are glorified chatbots, while the next generation, based on coding-agent breakthroughs, actually gets work done (executing multi-step workflows, orchestrating across systems, working autonomously with checkpoints).
- It explicitly frames its work on the 2024 coding-agent breakthrough (Claude Code, Cursor) now powering business agents (it references Claude and Microsoft Cowork and OpenClaw).
- It offers three services: Strategy (workflow assessment, use-case selection, architecture direction, deployment roadmap), Implementation (agent system design, tool and system integration, workflow orchestration, deployment and iteration), and Training (leadership workshops, technical enablement, team operating patterns). It runs engagements as "AI Agents Sprints," has a product referenced as "DataBox," and publishes case studies (CRM automation, quote automation, order processing/QC, an AI planning assistant).
- It is led by co-founders Matthias Lübken and Ivan Pedrazas, positioned as practitioners combining technical depth and enterprise experience, and it tracks agent harnesses, MCP and tool integration, and enterprise workflow automation.

So Tavon is a practitioner-led agentic-AI consultancy that scopes, builds, ships, and enables agent systems for businesses, grounded in the coding-agent paradigm.

## Why This Matters To SG/Send (A Different Kind Of Partner)

The other partners in this set are technical primitives SG/Vault integrates with. Tavon is different: it is a **channel and implementation partner**. This matters because much of SG/Vault's value is realised through implementation for specific businesses and use cases, which is exactly what Tavon does.

The alignment is strong and specific:

- **Same paradigm.** Tavon is built on the coding-agent breakthrough and tracks agent harnesses, MCP, and tool integration, the same world SG/Vault is built for (cross-ref: the agent-controlled-websites, skills, and Pi/Netlify briefs). They speak the same language.
- **Implementation is where vaults land.** SG/Vault's assessment, consulting, and creator-economy models (cross-ref: the consulting and base-vault briefs) all involve deploying tailored solutions for businesses; a firm like Tavon is who deploys them. The consulting brief's three delivery models (self-service, send-to-consultant, consultant-driven; cross-ref) explicitly include consultant-driven, and Tavon is a consultant.
- **Enterprise reach.** Tavon brings enterprise experience and client relationships, a channel SG/Vault does not have on its own.

So Tavon is less something SG/Vault integrates into the product, and more a partner who builds businesses and client solutions on top of SG/Vault.

## How SG/Send Could Work With Tavon

| Mode | How |
|------|-----|
| **Tavon deploys vault-based solutions** | Tavon uses SG/Vault as part of the agent systems it builds and ships for clients |
| **SG/Vault as the consultant-driven delivery substrate** | Tavon drives consulting assessments delivered as vaults (cross-ref: the consulting brief's consultant-driven model) |
| **Tavon as a creator in the ecosystem** | Tavon authors and sells base vaults / skills for its domains (cross-ref: the base-vault and marketplace briefs) |
| **Co-built case studies** | Tavon's case studies (CRM, quote, order automation) become vault examples on the library (cross-ref: the industry-pages and library briefs) |
| **Enablement uses vaults** | Tavon's training and enablement uses vaults as the curated, provable, shareable artefacts |
| **Provenance and trust for client work** | SG/Vault gives Tavon's client deliverables provenance, version control, and explainability (cross-ref: the base-vault and vault-per-standard briefs) |

The cleanest fit is **the consultant-driven delivery model**. The consulting brief (cross-ref) set out three delivery models, and the consultant-driven one is where an expert drives an assessment augmented by the workflow. Tavon is exactly such an expert firm: it could drive vault-based assessments and agent-system deployments for its clients, with SG/Vault providing the substrate (the curated, provable, shareable vault), the provenance and explainability (which enterprises need), and the artefacts. SG/Vault augments Tavon (cross-ref: the consulting brief's augment-the-human thesis): the firm brings the client relationship, the domain expertise, and the judgment; SG/Vault makes the deliverables curated, provable, and scalable.

## How It Helps Businesses Be Created Around It

This is the heart of the brief: **a firm like Tavon is precisely who builds businesses on SG/Vault.** The base-vault creator economy and the consulting models (cross-ref: the base-vault and consulting briefs) need practitioners who package expertise and deliver it to clients; Tavon is a worked example of that practitioner. So the relationship is two-directional:

- **SG/Vault helps Tavon build its business.** SG/Vault gives Tavon a substrate for provable, curated, shareable, scalable client deliverables; the base-vault model to package and sell its expertise; the marketplace to distribute it; and the provenance and explainability enterprises demand. Tavon can build more, prove more, and scale more with SG/Vault than without.
- **Tavon helps SG/Vault reach businesses.** Tavon brings the implementation capability, the enterprise relationships, and the on-the-ground use cases that turn SG/Vault's capabilities into deployed solutions and revenue. It is a channel to the businesses SG/Vault wants to reach.

This generalises: Tavon is one instance of a partner type, **agentic-AI implementation firms**, that SG/Vault should cultivate as both creators (in the ecosystem) and channels (to clients). The brief's recommendation is to treat firms like Tavon as a strategic partner class, not a one-off integration.

## What This Asks For

1. **Treat Tavon as a channel and implementation partner** (not a product integration), and the broader class of agentic-AI implementation firms as a strategic partner type.
2. **Offer SG/Vault as the consultant-driven delivery substrate** (cross-ref: the consulting brief), giving such firms provable, curated, shareable, scalable deliverables.
3. **Enable such firms as creators** (authoring and selling base vaults / skills for their domains; cross-ref: the base-vault and marketplace briefs).
4. **Co-build case studies** (their real implementations as vault examples on the library; cross-ref: the industry-pages and library briefs).
5. **Lead with provenance and explainability** (what enterprises need, and what SG/Vault provides over generic agent builds).
6. **Speak the shared paradigm** (coding-agent breakthrough, harnesses, MCP, skills; the common language).
7. **Produce the library page and example vault** (the page explains the partner/channel model; an example vault from a real or representative implementation is the MVP).

Estimated effort: this is a partnership and go-to-market motion, not a product build. The work is the partnership itself (positioning SG/Vault for implementation firms), the consultant-driven delivery support, and co-built case studies. The "MVP" is a worked example: a vault-based solution of the kind Tavon builds, presented as a case study.

## What This Does Not Try To Be

- **Not a product integration.** Tavon is a channel and implementation partner, not a technical primitive.
- **Not a replacement for SG/Vault's own go-to-market.** It is an additional channel through implementation firms.
- **Not a single deal.** It defines a strategic partner class (agentic-AI implementation firms).
- **Not generic agent consulting.** The differentiator is SG/Vault's provenance, curation, and provable deliverables.

## Honest Risks

**Risk 1: Channel partnerships need alignment and support.** Implementation partners need enablement and a reason to choose SG/Vault. Mitigation: lead with the differentiator (provenance, curation, scoring, provable deliverables); the base-vault/marketplace gives them a revenue model; co-built case studies.

**Risk 2: Dependence on partners for reach.** Relying on firms like Tavon for enterprise reach. Mitigation: cultivate a class of partners, not one; SG/Vault's direct channels (the library, the marketplace) operate in parallel.

**Risk 3: Quality and brand via partners.** Client work via partners reflects on SG/Vault. Mitigation: provenance and explainability make deliverables auditable; the marketplace scoring and reputation apply; choose aligned, practitioner-led partners.

**Risk 4: Confidentiality of client work.** Partner client deliverables are often confidential. Mitigation: the vault's zero-knowledge model and private vaults (cross-ref: the consulting brief's private/ephemeral options); public case studies are high-level and consented.
