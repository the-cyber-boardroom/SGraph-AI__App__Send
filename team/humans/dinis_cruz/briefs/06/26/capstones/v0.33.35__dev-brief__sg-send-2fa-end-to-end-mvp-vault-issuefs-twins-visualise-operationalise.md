# The 2FA End-to-End MVP: Building It on SG/Vault and IssueFS

**version** v0.33.35
**date** 26 June 2026
**from** Architect
**to** @Dev, Architect, Product, Strategy
**type** Dev brief

## What This Is

The plan for a full end-to-end MVP of the 2FA scenario, built on SG/Vault and IssueFS, rich enough to start exploring how to visualise and operationalise the whole model: **build the organisation first as a graph of digital twins in IssueFS, load the 2FA mappings as the data, run the risk movement through the twins with every graph-updating action performed by a twin bound to a human or an agent, route every change through a versioned IssueFS pipeline whose commit log is the narrative, query it with directed inward-and-outward paths and the wide-then-narrow multi-graph creation passes, and render story-driven, reproducible views; it is at once the demonstration and the core technology, and the 2FA scenario is deliberately rich enough to exercise the entire model on a single case.** It is the buildable synthesis of the v0.33.35 series (cross-ref: the 2FA semantic-graph, first-mappings, demo, query-paths, and path-properties briefs). New contributions: **the layered architecture, the build order, and the MVP acceptance criteria.**

## Why the 2FA Scenario Is Enough

One scenario exercises the whole model. Admin accounts without two-factor authentication gives a real, binary vulnerability, so it satisfies the facts-only rule. From it the model gets to demonstrate the no-deny acceptance with intervals, the distinction between the technical owner who validates and the business owner who owns, propagation to the CEO and the board, the recursive governance air-gap risk when the wrong person accepts, the cascade that fires and resolves, and the full confidentiality, integrity, and availability blast radius with GDPR, the ICO, the CFO, integrity tampering, and untested backups. It also exercises the twins, the agents that act, the directed query paths, and the narrative. Building this one case builds the product.

## The Architecture

The MVP is layered, each layer drawn from a brief in the series:

1. **Foundation, SG/Vault.** Zero-knowledge, PKI, provenance, version control. The data never leaves the keys.
2. **Store, IssueFS.** The graph lives as folders of issues, each issue a file with a definition, no database, queryable, operable by an agent through the CLI.
3. **Data, the 2FA mappings.** The node, edge, and acceptance instances, loaded from the mappings JSON.
4. **Change pipeline.** Every change is an IssueFS transformation, versioned, and cascades to the register; the commit log is the narrative.
5. **Twin and actor layer.** The organisation as fractal twins; every actor a twin bound to a human or an agent.
6. **Query and path engine.** Directed inward-and-outward paths, the path-query set, and the wide-then-narrow creation passes.
7. **Visualisation layer.** Story-driven, code-generated, reproducible, version-controlled views.

## Build Order, Bottom Up

**Phase 1: The organisation as twins, in IssueFS.** Build the company first, the HR system, the people, the roles, the actors, as a graph of twins stored in IssueFS, with abstraction layers aligned to the real handovers between systems. Load the 2FA mappings as the initial data.

**Phase 2: The risk movement and the change pipeline.** Wire the cascade: the vulnerability gives rise to the first risk, it propagates to the technical owner, the wrong-acceptor fact spawns the governance air-gap risk that travels to the board and then resolves, the risk re-homes to the business owner, and the blast radius blooms through confidentiality, integrity, and availability. Every step is a versioned IssueFS transformation, and the ordered transformations are the narrative.

**Phase 3: Twins as actors.** Make each role a twin bound either to a human or to an agent acting on the human's behalf. Have the agent actors create the actions, accept risks in a direction and for an interval, and document their reasoning, their choice of one option over another, and their persona.

**Phase 4: The query and path engine.** Implement the directed inward-and-outward paths and the first path-query set, so the graph answers reach, ownership, propagation, and air-gap queries, with multi-seed queries converging on the board rather than exploding. Capture the universe around the scenario in a wide first pass, then narrow it with queries, and render paths as readable sentences.

**Phase 5: Visualisation and the narrative.** Build the story views: replay the ordered changes as a narrative, like a commentator calling a match or a whodunit, shape a graph down to the few nodes that carry a message, and make every view code-generated, screenshot-able, and version-controlled. This is where the world-model and game-design thinking is explored.

**Phase 6: Operationalise.** Connect the twins to real systems where an API exists, the identity system such as Intune, Entra, or Okta for the 2FA state, and track every remaining gap explicitly. Turn the acceptances, intervals, underwriting, propagation, and pre-approval into a workflow that a real owner can drive.

## What to Explore

Because the scenario is rich, the MVP is also the sandbox for the open design questions: the best way to visualise a graph of graphs for a non-technical stakeholder; how to operationalise acceptance and propagation as a real workflow; the query syntax and whether saved paths become named lenses; how agent personas should reason and document; and how far to consolidate nodes for a story without distorting the truth.

## What This Does Not Try To Be

- **Not top down.** It builds the organisation first, then runs risk through it.
- **Not database-backed.** It lives in IssueFS as folders and files on the vault.
- **Not hypothetical.** Only facts and evidence are loaded.
- **Not the final UI.** It is rich enough to explore visualisation, not to settle it.

## Honest Tensions

| Tension | Note |

| Scope creep | Keep to the one 2FA scenario until it is end to end |

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The organisation exists as a graph of twins in IssueFS, no database.
2. The 2FA mappings load and validate against the ontology.
3. The cascade runs end to end and propagates to the board.
4. The governance air-gap risk spawns and then resolves when the risk re-homes.
5. The blast radius expands through confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
6. Every change is a versioned IssueFS transformation, and the log replays as narrative.
7. The path queries resolve, and multi-seed queries converge without exploding.
8. At least one actor is an agent twin that accepts a risk and documents its reasoning.
9. At least one twin connects to a real system, with any gap tracked.
10. Story views are code-generated, reproducible, and version-controlled.
