## What This Is

A first concrete set of mappings for the 2FA use case, the scenario instantiated as data against the ontology: **every real thing in the scenario mapped to a node of a defined class and every relationship mapped to a typed directed edge, covering the two systems, the two vulnerabilities, the nine risks, the supporting and challenging evidence, the actors and their org-chart lines, the asset and its data class, the attacker and the attack, the regulatory obligation, the three impact dimensions, and the acceptances with their directions, intervals, underwriters, and propagation; a set of the path queries from the previous brief is then resolved against these mappings to show they hold, and the same data is shipped as a machine-readable JSON.** It is the data layer for the worked example (cross-ref: the v0.33.35 2FA semantic-graph, query-paths, and risk-register briefs). New contributions: **the instance node set, the edge set, the acceptance set, and the resolved queries.** The machine-readable form is the companion file `v0.33.35__data__sg-send-2fa-mappings.json`.

## The Mapping Approach

Each real thing becomes a node of a class from the ontology, and each relationship becomes a directed edge with a known inverse. Nodes are thin; the meaning lives in the edges. Only facts and evidence are mapped, in keeping with the facts-only phase. Identifiers are stable so the mappings can be linked, queried, and versioned.

Once R3 is re-homed to ROLE-HRD, the condition behind R2 no longer holds, so R2 resolves and a change cascades up to clear it from the CIO, CEO, and Board views.

## Resolving the Path Queries

The queries from the query-paths brief, run against these mappings:

- **Q4, vulnerability to owners** `V1 -gives_rise_to-> Risk -owned_by-> Actor` resolves to risks {R1, R3} and owners {ROLE-HRD}, with R1 validated_by ROLE-ITD.
- **Q5, a risk's path to the board** `R3 -accepted_by-> Actor -reports_to*-> {Board}` resolves to ROLE-HRD, ROLE-CPO, ROLE-CEO, ROLE-BOARD.
- **Q6, an actor's accountability, inward** `ROLE-HRD <-owned_by- Risk` resolves to {R3, R4}, plus {R5, R9} by acceptance.
- **Q8, the confidentiality chain** `R3 -impacts-> {Confidentiality} -triggers-> Obligation -reported_to-> Actor` resolves to IMP-C, OBL-GDPR, BODY-ICO, with underwriters {ROLE-CFO, ROLE-CEO}.
- **Q9, full blast radius** `V1 -gives_rise_to-> Risk -impacts-> Impact` resolves through R3 to {IMP-C, IMP-I, IMP-A} and on to {R5, R6, R7, R8, R9}.
- **Q13, multi-seed convergence** `{V1, V2} -gives_rise_to-> Risk -owned_by-> Actor -reports_to*-> {CEO}` converges through {R1, R3} and {ROLE-HRD} onto ROLE-CEO and ROLE-BOARD, a bounded result despite two seeds.
- **Q14, air gaps** `Risk WHERE NOT -connected_to-> Register` resolves to the empty set here, every risk is on the register, which is the desired state.

These resolutions confirm the mappings traverse cleanly and converge as intended.

## What This Does Not Try To Be

- **Not exhaustive.** It is a first set; the five-whys chains and the integrity and availability branches can be deepened.
- **Not hypothetical.** Only facts and evidence are mapped.
- **Not final intervals.** The acceptance intervals are illustrative and owner-set.
- **Not a schema change.** It instantiates the existing ontology, it does not alter it.

1. Every node maps to a defined ontology class.
2. Every edge maps to a typed directed relationship with a known inverse.
3. Only facts and evidence are instantiated.
4. The acceptances carry direction, interval, underwriters, and propagation.
5. The path queries resolve against the mappings as expected.
6. The JSON validates against the ontology dictionary.
