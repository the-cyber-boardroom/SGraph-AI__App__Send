## Through-Lines

- **Risk, owned.** Surface it, accept it for an interval, underwrite it to the board; there is no deny button.
- **Facts only.** Phase one admits only real, existing vulnerabilities; the discipline of reality keeps the graph clean.
- **Meaning from connectivity.** Nodes are thin; the directed edges carry the meaning, and the paths read as language.
- **Convergence, not explosion.** Inward and outward paths converge on the natural peaks, so multi-seed queries stay bounded.
- **The story.** The register is experienced as a narrative, replayed through queries, timestamps, and commits.
- **The vault underneath.** Zero-knowledge, PKI, provenance, no database; sovereignty, no lock-in, agentic-native.
