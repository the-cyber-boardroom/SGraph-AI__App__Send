**date** 13 June 2026

## What This Is

Improvements to the existing LinkedIn intelligence UI: **a lighter theme, a Kanban board backed by Issues-FS (GitHub-issue-style cards, drag and drop, threads and comments, copy-paste, one board per agent), live schema and ontology visualisations in Mermaid, centres of gravity around people, companies, topics, and tasks, an outreach actions-and-communications workflow, and managing the tens of outreach vaults from within the UI.** It is the companion to the agentic-team setup (cross-ref: the v0.33.26 linkedin-agentic-team-vault-agents brief) and continues the LinkedIn cluster (cross-ref: the v0.33.16 linkedin-semantic-knowledge-graph-crm-outreach, semantic-graph-visualisation, company-intelligence-vault, and partner-outreach briefs). New contributions: **the Issues-FS-backed Kanban with per-agent boards, the live Mermaid schema and ontology visualisations, the centres-of-gravity model, and the in-UI outreach-vault management.**

## A Lighter Theme

The first and simplest ask: **the UI is too dark; make it much lighter.**

The project lead: **"it is quite dark, let's make it much more white."** A lighter theme for the LinkedIn intelligence UI (cross-ref: the UI-primitives theme work in the recipient-vault-experience-improvements brief, where theme is one of the three primitives).

## The Kanban Board

The central new surface: **a Kanban board, backed by Issues-FS, that the project lead uses to consume and communicate, with a board per agent.**

The project lead: **"we should add a Kanban board to track development and ideas, so everything I add here we have there. It is a good example of testing our environment. I should consume and communicate using a Kanban board. The typical thing of moving and dragging and dropping, creating new ones by entering, copy and pasting images and text, like a GitHub issue but in a board, with threads and comments. We should use Issues-FS, which already has a concept of issues and tasks. Each agent should have its own Kanban board, and when I want to review something I move it from test to the next column, or ask an agent to do it."**

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Backed by Issues-FS | Reuse the existing issues-and-tasks concept (cross-ref: the issues and task-registry work) |
| GitHub-issue-style cards | Cards with threads and comments, like an issue but on a board |
| Interactions | Drag and drop, create by entering, copy-paste images and text |
| One board per agent | Each agent has its own board |
| The review flow | Move cards between columns (for example test to review), or ask an agent to move them |
| Reuse a prior workflow | A previous project had a good Kanban workflow worth mapping in |

So the Kanban is both the project lead's working surface and a real-world test of the environment.

## Schema And Ontology Visualisations

Make the structure visible: **hyperlink and connect everything, and visualise the schemas and semantic ontologies, live, in Mermaid.**

The project lead: **"we need to start hyperlinking everything and connecting, creating visualisations of the schemas and the semantic ontologies of everything we have. For example, a visualisation of the LinkedIn data, what connects to what, what gaps we have, what the schemas are. This is where we use Mermaid, and it can be quite live. Let's map out how everything works and fits together."**

| Visualisation | Shows |
|---------------|-------|
| The LinkedIn data schema | What connects to what, and the gaps |
| The semantic ontologies | The structure the ontologist builds (cross-ref: the agentic-team brief) |
| How everything fits | The whole system, mapped and connected, live in Mermaid |

(Cross-ref: the semantic-graph-visualisation-subgraph-flip-verb-edges brief.)

## Centres Of Gravity

Organise the intelligence around anchors: **create centres of gravity around people, companies, relationships, topics, and tasks.**

The project lead: **"start creating big centres of gravity around people, companies, relationships, topics, and tasks or areas of focus. A good one is the need to reach out to the identity providers, the LastPass and the hyperscalers and all that, so start thinking about those, the actions, the to-do list, the communications, the messages I need to send."**

| Centre Of Gravity | Example |
|-------------------|---------|
| People | The contacts the analysis surfaces |
| Companies | Target organisations (cross-ref: the company-intelligence-vault brief) |
| Topics and areas of focus | For example the identity-provider outreach (LastPass, the hyperscalers; cross-ref: the partner-outreach briefs) |
| Tasks | The actions tied to each anchor |

## The Outreach Actions And Communications Workflow

From insight to action: **capture the actions per topic, and work out how to actually send the messages.**

The project lead: **"capture the actions we need to do on a particular topic. Then have a conversation about that topic, create a strategy, even create the vaults. We also need to think about how we send the messages, how the email flows, what messaging medium, whether I use LinkedIn, and how to make it easy for me to copy and paste all that."**

| Step | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Capture actions | The to-do list, communications, and messages per topic |
| Decide the medium | Email flow, messaging medium, or LinkedIn |
| Ease the sending | Make copy-paste into LinkedIn easy |
| Topic to strategy to vaults | A conversation on a topic becomes a strategy and the vaults that support it |

## Managing The Outreach Vaults From Within

Tie it back to vaults: **create and manage the tens of outreach vaults from inside the UI.**

The project lead: **"we are going to be creating tens of vaults, and now we have a nice feature to create vaults, so we should manage the vaults from in here, control them, and have the workflow. Eventually it will be powerful when the tools and agents on the vault itself help me with these actions, but a lot of these the main Claude session can already do."** So the UI becomes the place to create, see, and manage the per-person and per-topic vaults (cross-ref: the recipient-created-disposable-vault create-vault feature and the agentic-team brief for in-vault agents that will eventually assist).

## What This Asks For

1. **Lighten the theme** (much more white).
2. **Add the Issues-FS-backed Kanban** (GitHub-issue-style, drag and drop, threads and comments, copy-paste, one board per agent).
3. **Build live schema and ontology visualisations** (Mermaid; the LinkedIn data, the gaps, the ontologies).
4. **Create the centres of gravity** (people, companies, topics, tasks).
5. **Build the outreach actions and communications workflow** (capture actions, choose the medium, ease LinkedIn copy-paste, topic to strategy to vaults).
6. **Manage the outreach vaults from within the UI** (create, see, and control the tens of vaults).

Estimated effort: large in aggregate, sequence-able; the lighter theme and the Kanban are the near-term wins, the visualisations and outreach workflow build on the semantic graph and the agentic team.

## What This Does Not Try To Be

- **Not a scraper-driven feed.** It visualises the exported data (cross-ref: the agentic-team brief's terms stance).
- **Not fully automated outreach.** The project lead drives the actions; copy-paste is eased, not automated.
- **Not dependent on in-vault agents yet.** The main Claude session does much of it now.

## Honest Risks

**Risk 1: scope.** Many features at once would stall. Mitigation: theme and Kanban first.

**Risk 2: the Kanban is also a test.** It doubles as an environment test, so issues there are expected. Mitigation: treat it as dogfooding.

**Risk 3: visualisations can overwhelm.** A live graph of everything can become noise. Mitigation: centres of gravity give it structure.

**Risk 4: outreach must stay within terms and tasteful.** Mitigation: eased copy-paste, not bulk automation; the project lead reviews.

**Risk 5: tens of vaults need management.** Mitigation: manage from the UI; the create-vault feature exists.
