## Notes (carry to the adviser)

- The single most important clause is the company/offer separation: *"this openness applies to the company and the product; the investment opportunity itself is not offered to the general public."* That is what lets you lead with transparency without widening the financial promotion.
- The biggest compliance lever is **who sees the invitation** (FSMA s.21 high-net-worth / sophisticated-investor exemptions), i.e. the gating — not the wording alone.
- The transparency claim is credible because it is **backed** (the CC BY 4.0 corpus, the public briefs, rendering from a public vault). Flag to the adviser specifically that you intend to lead with openness, so they can confirm the company/offer separation holds.
- This is **not legal advice**; these are compliance-ready drafts for qualified-adviser review before going live.
