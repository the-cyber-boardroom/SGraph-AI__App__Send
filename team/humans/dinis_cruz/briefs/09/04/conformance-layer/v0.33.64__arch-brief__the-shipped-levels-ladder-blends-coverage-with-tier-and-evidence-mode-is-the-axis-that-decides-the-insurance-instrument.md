# The Shipped Levels Ladder Blends Coverage With Tier And Has No Evidence-Mode Axis At All: How A Control Is Verified Decides Which Insurance Instrument It Can Be Rather Than Merely What It Costs, The Layer Already Separated Two Of The Three Axes Onto The Assertion, And Level One Should Be Whatever This Organisation Has Already Commoditised

**version** v0.33.64
**date** 4 September 2026
**from** Human (project lead)
**to** Engineering, Product, Design

**type** Arch brief

*Fourth of 4 September, reissued. The conformance layer ships a levels ladder of nought to five, derived per control from its own requirements and computed rather than asserted, which is the right construction and is not a maturity model in the sense the memo asks for. This brief is a critique of that ladder and an extension of it, written after reading it rather than proposing into a void. The layer has already separated two of the three axes argued for here, putting granularity and observability on the assertion, which is the strongest evidence that the third belongs there too. Limitation: the mapping from evidence mode to insurance instrument in the middle of this brief is the sharpest claim in it and it is a proposal by analogy to how cover is written, not a position anybody underwriting has agreed to.*

---

## What This Is

An argument that the shipped ladder is measuring two things at once and missing a third: **the layer derives a level from the requirements each control already has, one core requirement evidenced is level one, every core is two, plus a supplemental is three, every requirement is four, and four with every attestation at the observed or third-party tier is five, which is honest, computed and uneven in a way the layer states rather than smooths, and it is also two axes multiplied into one integer, since levels one to four move on coverage of requirements while level five alone moves on the tier of the evidence, so a jump from four to five is a different kind of jump from every other and the number cannot say which kind it was; the layer already knows this, because two things the specification could not name have been given fields on the assertion rather than states, with granularity carried by a vacuous marker naming the layer at which an attestation holds and its reason, and observability carried by an outside-only marker naming what a subject cannot see about itself, which is precisely the treatment argued for here and leaves one axis still folded into the number; the axis that is missing entirely is evidence mode, the thing the memo names, because the same control asserted verbally, written into a policy, checked by hand on a cadence, computed programmatically from a record, or enforced inline at the moment of the action are five materially different things and the word evidence mode does not appear anywhere in the layer, while the ladder's only nod to it is the tier field at level five; the mode matters more than a price, because it decides what kind of instrument a control can be at all, with asserted and documented supporting a disclosure whose failure is misrepresentation rather than a claim, a manual check supporting a rating factor and never a condition because nobody is present at the action, an out-of-band computation supporting a warranty whose breach is provable after the loss, and only an inline check supporting a condition precedent, which is the only mode that can justify lifting an exclusion since it is the only one where the answer to whether the thing could have happened is no; the mode carries the corpus's own sub-test, that a check the actor can disable is not inline, and the layer's own architecture shows both sides of it, since its write scoping is refused by the host outside the app's reach and its read surface is refused by the app about itself; and the memo's second idea, that level one should be whatever the organisation has already commoditised, makes the ladder local and therefore not portable, which is the point, because a ladder that pretends two organisations start in the same place has told neither of them anything.** New contributions in this reissue: **the shipped ladder read as coverage times tier, the two axes the layer already moved onto the assertion, evidence mode as the third, the instrument mapping, the enforcer sub-test with the layer's own architecture as its worked example, and the local anchoring rule.**

## What The Ladder Does Today

```
   0   no requirement evidenced, or any requirement contradicted
   1   at least one core requirement evidenced with an unexpired attestation
   2   every core requirement evidenced with an unexpired attestation
   3   level 2, and at least one supplemental requirement evidenced
   4   every requirement evidenced with an unexpired attestation
   5   level 4, and every attestation at the observed or third-party tier
```

Four things it gets right, and they should not be lost in what follows. **It is derived from the standard's own requirements rather than invented.** **It is computed rather than asserted.** **It is uneven and says so**, since fourteen controls have no supplemental requirement and skip three, and eleven have a single requirement so one and two coincide. And **freshness is already in it**, because an expired attestation stops counting without anybody editing anything.

## The Ladder Is Coverage, Times Tier At The Top

Levels nought to four move on one question: **how many of this control's requirements are evidenced?** Level five moves on a different one: **how good is the evidence?**

So a step from one to two and a step from four to five are not the same kind of step, and the integer cannot say which happened. Two subjects at level four have the same coverage and unknown evidence quality; one at five and one at four may differ by a single tier on a single attestation.

**The layer's own findings show the consequence.** Every supporting attestation in the first build is self-reported, and the one observed-tier artefact is a contradiction, so **level five is unreachable for every one of the fifty-three controls**. An axis that is unreachable across an entire estate is not doing the work of an axis; it is a ceiling. Separating it would let a subject be reported as fully covered at a weak tier, which is the true and useful statement the current ladder cannot make.

## The Layer Already Separated Two Of Three

This is the strongest argument for the third, because it is the layer's own precedent.

| Axis | Where it lives now | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Coverage** | The level, one to four | In the number |
| **Granularity** | A `vacuous` marker on the requirement, with its layer and its reason | **On the assertion**, shown and never counted |
| **Observability** | An `outside-only` marker with its reason, while the state stays unevidenced | **On the assertion** |
| **Evidence mode** | **Nowhere.** The tier field at level five is its only trace | Still folded in |

The reasoning that put granularity and observability on the assertion applies unchanged to mode: each is a property of how a claim was arrived at rather than of how much was claimed, and each can be true while the coverage is unchanged. **A requirement can go from a colleague's assurance to an enforced refusal without a single additional requirement being evidenced**, and today that improvement is invisible.

## Evidence Mode, As The Third Axis

| Rung | Mode | When it is known | How long can it be broken before anybody knows? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Asserted | At the moment of asking | Indefinitely |
| **1** | Documented | At the moment of writing | Indefinitely. **A document is not a control** |
| **2** | Manually checked on a cadence | At each check | Until the next check |
| **3** | Programmatic, out of band | Shortly after the action | Briefly, and the record proves it |
| **4** | Programmatic, inline | Before the action | **Not at all.** A failure is a refusal and a refusal is an event |

**The rung measures how long a control can be broken before anybody knows**, which is why it is a different question from how many requirements are covered.

### The sub-test that prevents rung four inflation

> **An inline check the actor can disable, bypass, reconfigure or unset is not inline.** It is rung three at best, because the actor's compliance is doing the work.

One question decides it: **can the thing being controlled turn off the thing controlling it?** And the operational version, for anybody who cannot read the code: **a control whose tightening cannot break anything was not in the path.**

The layer's own chat is the cleanest worked example in the estate, because it has both sides at once:

| Surface | Enforced by | Actor can reach the enforcer? | Rung |
|---|---|---|---|
| Writes, scoped to one directory | The host, per the manifest | **No** | **4** |
| Read surface, via the app's own path check | The app | Yes, it is the app | 3 |

Same feature, same page, two rungs. **A single number for that chat's tool-call control would be a lie in one direction or the other.**

## The Instrument Mapping

This is the direct connection the memo asks for, and it is stronger than a discount. **The evidence mode decides what kind of thing a control can be in a policy at all.**

| Rung | What it can be | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **0 Asserted** | A **disclosure** on the proposal form | If wrong, the remedy is misrepresentation, not a claim. It bounds nothing |
| **1 Documented** | A disclosure, and perhaps a small rating factor | A document evidences intent, and intent predicts frequency weakly |
| **2 Manually checked** | A **rating factor** | It reduces expected frequency, which is what a premium prices. It cannot gate an action because nobody is present at one |
| **3 Out of band** | A **warranty**, with provable breach, an excess, and experience rating that bites | Breach is provable from the record. The loss has happened when breach is found, so it caps severity rather than preventing occurrence |
| **4 Inline** | A **condition precedent**, and the only rung that can justify **lifting an exclusion** | The insurer knows the action cannot occur. The only mode where the answer is no rather than probably not |

**The last row is the commercially important one.** An exclusion exists because a loss class is uninsurable, usually because it is irreversible, and the only honest reason to lift one is that the loss can no longer occur. Everything else earns a better price; **rung four changes what is covered.**

Two consequences. **A rung-two control cannot be a condition however well run**, and writing it as one produces a policy that cannot be tested at the moment it matters. And **moving one control from rung three to rung four can be worth more than moving ten from rung one to rung two**, which is the opposite of how these programmes are usually resourced.

The layer's insurability query already emits conditions and exclusions. Adding the mode axis makes that emission principled rather than uniform: today a met condition is a met condition; with mode it is a warranty or a condition precedent, and the exclusions it can lift follow.

## Level One Is Whatever This Organisation Has Already Commoditised

The memo's second idea. There is no point saying level one is something nobody knows how to do, and no point saying level two is something the organisation already has.

```
   1   what this organisation already does everywhere, reliably, without being asked
   2   what it does in some places and could do everywhere with effort it can name
   3   what it can do once, with a project
   4   what it cannot do today
```

**Level one is free by construction**, so an assessment starts with a true statement rather than an aspirational one. **The ladder is not portable, and that is the point**: an organisation with a mature pipeline has continuous integration at level one and one without has it at level three, and a ladder that pretends otherwise has told neither of them anything.

Note what this does not do. It does not replace the shipped ladder, which is derived from the standard and belongs to the standard. **It is a second ladder, local, sitting beside it**, and a subject's report should carry both: how much of this control is evidenced, and where this control sits against what we already do.

**This is the third time this estate has arrived at the same shape.** The passive-mode derivation produces a curve and a person picks the draw rate. The policy proposes bands and a person picks the friction. Here the standard supplies the requirements and the organisation supplies the anchor. In each case the tool computes the space and a person chooses the point, and it is worth naming that pattern once rather than rediscovering it a fourth time.

## Composition Up The Fractal

The memo describes maturity models of maturity models, and the layer already inherits attestations from a team to the agent it operates. What neither supplies is the rule for rolling up, and the obvious choice is wrong.

> **Along a barrier path, take the minimum. Across independent paths, aggregate with exposure weights. Never average across a population.**

A division averaging level three with one team at level one whose agent reaches production is not at level three for any risk running through that team. **The bow ties supply the paths**, and a barrier chain is as strong as its weakest barrier, which is a minimum.

Two supporting rules. **A rollup always names its worst contributor.** And **composition is per risk rather than global**, because the same estate composes differently for a disclosure risk than for a spend risk.

## Context, Which Decides What A Level Means

| Dimension | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Capability** | Selects which controls apply. Already an edge, and already needing an authored class map |
| **Environment** | **Staging and production are different subjects**, not one subject at two levels |
| **Assets reachable** | Sets the consequence side, and therefore whether an exclusion matters |
| **Jurisdiction** | Selects which crosswalked obligations bind, which is where the article resolution earns its keep |
| **Threat landscape** | Sets expected frequency, the other half of the premium |
| **Team** | The subject, which the layer already asserts per team |

**Environment is the one most often collapsed**, and an organisation reporting one number across staging and production has averaged the place where nothing matters with the place where everything does.

## What This Must Not Do

- **Not replace the derived ladder.** It belongs to the standard and is correctly built; the local ladder sits beside it.
- **Not emit a single number without the axes beside it.**
- **Not accept a rung-four claim without a named enforcer** and an answer to whether the actor can reach it.
- **Not average maturity across a population.**
- **Not treat staging and production as one subject.**
- **Not present the instrument mapping as agreed underwriting practice.**

## What This Does Not Try To Be

- **Not a scoring scheme.** No weights, no index, and deliberately no single number to benchmark on.
- **Not an amendment to the standard.** A layer above a layer above a derived catalogue.
- **Not an underwriting manual.** The instrument mapping is the argument to have with an underwriter, not its outcome.
- **Not a claim that rung four is always right.** Most controls should sit at two or three, and the model exists to say which few are worth four.
- **Not a replacement for the bow tie.** It supplies each barrier's strength; the bow tie supplies the paths.

## Honest Tensions

| Tension | Note |
|---------|------|
| Splitting coverage from tier | It makes both sayable and it removes the single number people came for |
| A third axis on the assertion | It follows the layer's own precedent and it makes an assertion a paragraph rather than a row |
| The tightening test | It is the clearest signal available and it asks a team to prove a control by risking an outage |
| A local ladder beside a derived one | It makes level one true and it means two organisations cannot be compared, which benchmarking will resist |
| Minimum along a path | It is correct and it lets one weak team set a division's number |
| The instrument mapping | It is the sharpest connection here and an analogy until an underwriter agrees to it |

## Open Questions

| Question | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Is a hook that fails closed but lives where the agent can write it rung three or four? | The most common real case, and the enforcer test says three, which will surprise people |
| Does mode belong on the attestation or the requirement? | On the attestation, since two attestations for one requirement can differ in mode |
| How is coverage enumerated at scale? | Naming every exception works for tens and not thousands, and the answer probably involves the registry |
| Does the local ladder need a portable projection? | Somebody will want to compare, and any projection reintroduces the problem it solves |
| Should preventative-with-retrospective-evidence be flagged automatically? | Forty-seven of fifty-three are preventative and the flag is computable today |
| Is independence a fourth axis? | Third-party evaluation appears in the standard, and independence may not reduce to mode |
