**from** Human (project lead)
**to** Engineering, Product

*First of 4 September, reissued. The specification in this document was written in the morning from a voice memo and against the catalogue vault; the conformance layer was built from it the same day and published as a fork, and this document is reissued to describe what exists rather than what was proposed, because a plan left standing beside its implementation sends readers to the wrong one. ## What This Is

A record of the conformance layer as built, and of what remains: **the specification asked for one thing above all, that the edge answering does the standard say this be kept permanently apart from the edge answering does this subject do this, and the build implements exactly that with `evidenced_by` staying in the catalogue's graph and a new `attested_by` living in the layer's own, held apart by a test that goes red if a layer edge ever reaches a source observation, which is stronger than the specification asked for because it makes the rule executable rather than editorial; the layer sits above a byte-identical copy of the catalogue whose own twenty-one tests still pass including the one that rebuilds every source document to the word, it seeds one team across all fifty-three controls with unevidenced as the default state, and it produces at build time two evidenced, forty-eight unevidenced and three contradicted, one condition met and none of five consequences covered, which is an unflattering first result and is the correct one; the crosswalk work landed partially and its shortfall is the most useful number in the build, since only 62 of 1,126 published crosswalks target the EU AI Act at all and the other 1,064 point at twelve frameworks with no published graph, so they remain strings with anchors, and of the 62 that resolved every one resolves at article rather than paragraph level while eight of the twenty-seven target articles have since been amended by the omnibus regulation, which means the published crosswalk was written against text that has moved; the build settled an open question the specification left, by putting the inability to observe a requirement from inside on the assertion as an observability field with a reason rather than inventing a fourth state, and it added a vacuous marker carrying the granularity and the reason so that a coarse attestation is shown and never counted; three claims in the specification were wrong and the build found each of them, that the agent joins the standard hand-free through the capability edge when in fact seven capability classes must be mapped by hand onto twelve tool capabilities, that bow ties could draw recovery barriers from the standard when the standard has no recovery class at all, and that the counts published on the vault's own page were current when the vault holds more than twice the nodes and three times the edges; and what remains is now specific rather than general, being the 1,064 unresolved crosswalks, paragraph-level resolution that is possible and manual, a level five that no control can reach while every supporting attestation is self-reported, and a policy shape question that belongs to the insurance pack rather than to this layer.** New contributions in this reissue: **the two-edge rule stated as a test rather than a principle, the crosswalk shortfall and the amendment finding, the observability and vacuous fields as the build settled them, the three corrections, and a remaining-work list drawn from measurements rather than from intent.**

## The One Rule, As A Test

| Edge | Lives in | Answers | Absent means |
|---|---|---|---|
| `evidenced_by` | The catalogue's graph, untouched | Does the standard say this? | A build defect |
| `attested_by` | The layer's own graph | Does this subject do this? | **The control is unevidenced, which is the finding** |

The two are never traversed in one query without the query naming which it uses, and the layer holds that with `test_two_edge_rule`, which fails if a layer edge ever reaches a source observation. **A rule with a test is a rule; a rule in a document is a preference.** That is the single most important thing about the build and it should be the first thing anybody reads.

The layer sits above the catalogue and modifies none of it. The catalogue's own twenty-one tests still pass, including the one that rebuilds every source document byte-identically, and the layer adds seventeen of its own.

## The Conformance Object, As Built

```
conformance/v1  { subject {kind, ref}, control, release, as_of,
                  state: evidenced | contradicted | unevidenced,
                  level 0..5, max_level,
                  requirements { id: { state, application, tiers,
                                       observability, cannot_observe_because, vacuous } },
                  attested_by [{attestation, requirement, relation, tier,
                                fresh_at_as_of, inherited_from}],
                  required_level, met, valid_until, asserted_by, asserted_at }
```

Two fields exist that the specification did not name, and both are better than what it proposed.

**`observability`, with `cannot_observe_because`.** The specification asked whether the inability to evidence a requirement from inside was a fourth state or a tier. The build settles it as neither: it is a property of the subject and the observer together, so it sits on the assertion with a reason while the state stays `unevidenced`. That is right, and it means the vocabulary of states remains three words.

**`vacuous`, with its granularity and reason.** An attestation that supports a requirement at a granularity coarser than the risk it bounds is recorded on the assertion, shown, and never counted toward a level. It is not deleted and it is not credited, which is the honest treatment of a true statement that does not hold where it matters.

Levels are derived from the requirements each control already has rather than invented: one core requirement evidenced is level 1, every core is 2, plus a supplemental is 3, every requirement is 4, and 4 with every attestation at the observed or third-party tier is 5. Fourteen controls have no supplemental requirement and skip 3; eleven have a single requirement so their 1 and 2 coincide. **The ladder is derived and it is uneven, and saying so is better than smoothing it.**

## What The First Build Says

For the seeded team's agent, across fifty-three engaged controls at build time:

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Evidenced | 2 |
| Unevidenced | 48 |
| Contradicted | 3 |
| Conditions met in the emitted policy | **1** |
| Consequences covered | **0 of 5** |
| Acceptances stale | **3 of 3** |

And the demonstration the specification asked for, working: **between build time and 15 January 2027, with nothing edited by anybody, the single condition expires and the policy has fifty-three exclusions.** Insurability changes because evidence aged, not because a document was rewritten. That was claim twelve of the specification and it is now a report rather than a claim.

The three acceptances are all stale because they were signed on 1 September against barriers that were unevidenced, and attestations written since have moved some of those barriers. **An acceptance does not follow its evidence**, and the view of acceptances whose evidence has moved is, as predicted, the most useful thing the layer produces.

## The Crosswalk Work, And Its Shortfall

The specification called this the highest-value step and said the interesting number would be how many failed to resolve. It was right about the value and the number is worse than expected.

| Finding | Number |
|---|---|
| Published crosswalks in the catalogue | 1,126 |
| Targeting the EU AI Act | **62** |
| Resolved to article node ids with CELEX and hash | 62, all of them |
| Remaining as strings with anchors | **1,064**, across twelve frameworks with no published graph |
| Resolved at paragraph level | **0.** The catalogue's anchors carry no paragraph |
| Target articles amended since the crosswalk was published | **8 of 27**, by the omnibus regulation |

Three things follow. **The join is real and small.** Five and a half percent of the crosswalks reach a graph, because the estate has published one regulation and the catalogue points at thirteen frameworks.

**Paragraph-level resolution is possible and manual**, because the regulation graph has the paragraphs and the catalogue's anchors do not. That is a bounded piece of work on 62 rows rather than an open-ended one.

**And the amendment finding is the kind only a graph produces.** Eight target articles have been amended since the crosswalk was written, which no reader of either document alone would notice, and which makes a published mapping partially stale without anybody having made a mistake.

## Three Corrections The Build Made To The Specification

Recorded because the specification is superseded by them rather than because they are embarrassing.

**The agent join is not hand-free.** The specification claimed an agent reaches the standard through `applies_to_capability` without a mapping anybody maintains. In fact that edge points at seven capability classes while a measured grant lists twelve tool capabilities, so an authored class map is required and lives in the layer's terms. The join is still better than a control-by-control mapping and it is not free.

**The standard has no recovery class.** The specification proposed bow ties with preventive and recovery barriers drawn from controls. The catalogue classifies all fifty-three as preventative or detective and has no recovery category, so the recovery side of each bow tie is the layer's own reading of the failure-plan controls in the accountability domain. That reading should be marked as the layer's, not the standard's, wherever it appears.

**The counts on the published page were stale when the specification quoted them.** The page said 1,238 nodes and 3,526 edges; the vault holds 2,788 and 11,610 across twenty-six edge types. The specification took its figures from a page rather than from the vault, which is the same failure that led to this whole set being reissued, and the build's own findings caught it independently.

## What This Must Not Do

- **Not merge the two edges**, and not remove the test that enforces it.
- **Not inherit an authority the catalogue disclaims.** The layer says unofficial and derivative, and everything above it must too.
- **Not treat an absent assertion as compliance.** Unevidenced is the default and it is a state.
- **Not present the recovery barriers as the standard's classification.**
- **Not resolve a crosswalk by forcing it.** Report the 1,064 rather than guessing at them.
- **Not let an acceptance follow its evidence.** Stale is a finding, not a refresh.

## What This Does Not Try To Be

- **Not a certification.** It records what is evidenced and what is not, and certifies nobody.
- **Not an official mapping.** The crosswalks are the catalogue's, resolved to identifiers, and disagreements are findings.
- **Not an underwriting decision.** The query emits conditions and exclusions; writing cover is a person's call.
- **Not a complete conformance picture.** One team, one agent, one release, and every supporting attestation self-reported.
- **Not a replacement for the catalogue.** A directory above it that touches none of it.
