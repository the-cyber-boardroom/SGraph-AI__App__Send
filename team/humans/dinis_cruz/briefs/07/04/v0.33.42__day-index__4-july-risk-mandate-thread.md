## The Through-Line

The six briefs are one argument about a legible, value-driven platform. The commercial and go-to-market briefs decide how value is priced, discovered, and paid for, and the platform model is the day's anchor: primitives at the foundation, products as the surface, consumption as the engine, and the open, run-anywhere posture turning enterprise demands into the customer's own responsibility. The risk-cards and game-engine briefs make the value visible and visceral so people reach the point of paying. And the credibility loop turns the same visibility discipline inward, so the system learns whose statements to trust. Across all of them the pattern holds: show the value, ground it in evidence, make it legible, and let the customer own what is theirs.
