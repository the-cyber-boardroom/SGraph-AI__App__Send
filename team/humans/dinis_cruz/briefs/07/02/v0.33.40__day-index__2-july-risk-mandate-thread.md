## The Through-Line

The six briefs and the roadmap form one argument about accountable decisions. The question-engine and scenario briefs are about forcing the right questions and making the stakes felt. The near-miss and Gen AI paradox briefs are about what a good decision even means, counting the luck, and scaling legibility rather than opacity. The authorization brief defines the real scope that has to be accepted, and the RAMM makes maturity computable and interoperable rather than a questionnaire. The roadmap then shows that every one of these ships as customisation, vault, connector, or content work on the core that already exists.
