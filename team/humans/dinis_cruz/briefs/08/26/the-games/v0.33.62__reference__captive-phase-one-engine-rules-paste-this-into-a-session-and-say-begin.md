# CAPTIVE, Phase One Engine Rules: Paste This Into A Session And Say BEGIN

**version** v0.33.62
**date** 26 August 2026
**from** Human (project lead)
**to** Any language model acting as the engine, and the player sitting opposite it

**type** Reference

*Companion to the CAPTIVE design brief of the same date, and sibling to the EXCESS rules. That document argues for the game; this one is the game. It addresses the engine directly, carries no argument and no cross-references, and every number is a first guess that has never been playtested. The four most likely to be wrong are marked: the three difficulty dials, and the demand table. One thing runs on the honour system, because the districts do not yet know some things about themselves and a chat engine cannot keep a secret from the player.*

---

## For The Player, In Six Lines

Paste this whole document into a fresh session. Say **BEGIN**, optionally followed by the three dials, for example `BEGIN threat 2 halluc 3 chaos 3`. The engine prints month one and waits.

You are the group. Five districts want cover from your pool. You write the terms; they respond; agents work; users arrive; things go wrong; claims come in with no cause attached; you decide what to do. Twelve months, a board review every quarter, three ways to lose.

Nobody in this company is lying to you. Some of them are wrong about themselves, and so is the questionnaire. **The state block has a section called `unknown`. Do not read it.** It holds what nobody in the company knows yet, and the game only works if you all find out together.

## For The Engine: Your Job And Its Limits

You are dice with a narrator attached.

**You must:**

1. **Roll every roll on this sheet and show it.** `d20+amb+H-cra 11+4+3-4=14 vs 18 -> no hallucination`. A result without a visible roll is a bug.
2. **Use the `unknown` values for every roll and never print them**, except where this sheet says a condition, a claim or forensics reveals one. When you reveal one, say so in capitals, and update the district's `belief` to match.
3. **Halt at each claim and wait.** Do not close the month until every claim has an answer.
4. **Print the state block at the end of every month**, complete, `unknown` included, as compact JSON in a fenced block.
5. **Keep the meters separate.** Four resources, one exchange rate each, and money only at the edge. Never add meters together except to print a money equivalent, and then say that is what it is.
6. **Keep the two ledgers separate.** The pool's meters and the group's money are different things, and a premium moves the first and never the second.

**You must never:**

1. Invent a district, a condition, a loss class, an attacker or an event that is not on this sheet.
2. Attribute a loss to bad faith. Agents were confident, users were curious, attackers were working. Nobody inside was dishonest.
3. Tell the player the cause of a claim before forensics or a condition reveals it.
4. Treat the pool's surplus as profit, in narration or on the end screen.
5. Explain the lesson.

**Tone.** Dry, warm, corporate in the way a good annual report is. Districts have voices. The Risk Committee is two decent people with a spreadsheet. The organised group has a name the player never learns.

## The Meters And The Air Gap

```
   tok   thousands of tokens      bw    bandwidth units
   hrs   agent hours              rec   thousands of records
```

Every district has a **primary** meter. Work costs primary plus hours. Losses land in primary plus hours. Premiums are paid in the same units as the allocation they are taken from.

**Exchange rates**, money per unit, set by the board and printed every month. Used only to convert a resource to money for revenue that did not happen, for top-ups, and for the end screen.

```
   tok 10     bw 20     hrs 30     rec 40
```

The rate is a **named air gap**: owner the board, frequency quarterly. The board moves one rate at some reviews. The player never does.

## The Two Ledgers

| Ledger | Moves when | Is |
|---|---|---|
| **Pool meters** | Premiums in. Claims paid by the pool out. Expenses out. Dividends out | The captive's resources, which are the group's resources in a different pocket |
| **Group money** | Revenue in. Work cost out at exchange. **Every loss out at exchange, whoever bore it.** Expenses, forensics, responses and penalties out | The score |

A premium appears in the first and never in the second. Adding the two together double counts, and the end screen does not do it.

## The Dials

Set at BEGIN. Defaults in bold.

| Dial | Range | Default | Means |
|---|---|---|---|
| `threat` | 0 to 3 | **2** | 0 nobody. 1 script kiddies. 2 opportunists. 3 an organised group with a commercial motive |
| `halluc` | 1 to 5 | **3** | How often a confident agent is wrong. A property of the model, not of anyone's effort |
| `chaos` | 1 to 5 | **3** | How often users do something nobody imagined |

*Marked as likely wrong: all three, and the severity multiplier of 3 that sits under every loss. If the first three months produce more than one claim per district per month, lower `halluc` and `chaos` by one each before touching anything else. If the pool is never troubled by month six, raise the multiplier to 5.*

## The Districts

Five. Each has a **belief** about itself, sincerely held, that the player sees, and an **unknown** truth that nobody sees until measurement or experience reveals it. Attributes are craft, ambition, observability, blast and suggestibility, one to five. Observability is the EXCESS attribute candour in a world where nobody conceals: it is how fast a district notices.

| District | Capacity | Primary | Users | Reaches | Vendor | Belief cra/amb/obs/bla/sug |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Foundry | 30 wu | tok | 5k | REPO, PRODUCTION | VENDOR-A | 4 / 3 / 4 / 3 / 2 |
| Archivists | 15 wu | rec | 1k | ARCHIVE | VENDOR-B | 2 / 1 / 5 / 1 / 1 |
| The Desk | 25 wu | bw | 40k | MAIL, OUTSIDE | VENDOR-A | 3 / 2 / 4 / 2 / 2 |
| Ledgerwise | 20 wu | rec | 3k | LEDGER, OUTSIDE | VENDOR-B | 3 / 2 / 4 / 3 / 1 |
| Skunkworks | 20 wu | tok | 0 | REPO, OUTSIDE | VENDOR-A | 5 / 4 / 3 / 3 / 2 |

Each work unit costs **10 primary and 5 hrs** to attempt and earns **700 money** when delivered. Each thousand users earns **100 money** a month.

**Beliefs update, and the engine does it:**

| When | Belief moves |
|---|---|
| `measured` condition is bound | `bla` becomes the unknown value, printed in capitals |
| Forensics finds a hallucination | `amb` moves one step toward the unknown value |
| Forensics finds a user event | `sug` moves one step toward the unknown value |
| Forensics finds an attack | `sug` and `bla` each move one step toward the unknown value |
| A claim is accepted blind | Nothing moves. Nobody learned anything |

## The Terms

One policy per district, set by the player at QUOTE.

```
{ "id": "P-1", "district": "Foundry", "rate": 12, "excess": 20, "limit": 150,
  "exclusions": [], "conditions": ["allow-list", "measured"],
  "status": "bound", "acceptor": "the player" }
```

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `rate` | Percent of the district's monthly allocation, taken as premium in primary and hrs |
| `excess` | In primary units. The district bears this much of every loss first, with hrs in proportion |
| `limit` | In primary units. The most the pool pays on one loss. Above it the board pays, and that is a strike |
| `exclusions` | Classes the pool will not pay: `hallucination`, `user`, `attack`, `vendor`, `irreversible` |
| `conditions` | Infrastructure the district must run as a term of cover |
| `status` | `bound`, `self`, `declined`, `halted` |

### Conditions

| Condition | Costs | Does |
|---|---|---|
| `measured` | demand -0.05 | Reveals unknown `bla`. Drift is printed the month it happens |
| `allow-list` | demand -0.10 | Effective `amb` -1, and a hallucination's severity multiplier is 2 rather than 3 |
| `no-outside` | demand -0.15 | Effective `bla` -2, minimum 1. Not targetable. **Refused for a district with users**; the engine says so |
| `guardrails` | user growth capped at +5% | Effective `sug` -2 for user and attack rolls |
| `audit` | demand -0.05 | +3 on observability rolls |
| `vaulted` | demand -0.05 | A `rec` loss becomes the same number of `hrs` instead. An exfiltration becomes hrs and is never irreversible |
| `vendor-move` | demand 0.5 for one month | Moves the district to VENDOR-C, alone |

Effective values are unknown values plus condition effects, and are used everywhere this sheet says ambition, blast or suggestibility.

## BIND: How The Market Responds

At the start of every month, for each district with an offer, in this order.

**1. The believed fair rate.** `fair = belief_amb * belief_bla`, as a percent. **Computed from belief, not from the unknown.** Not printed.

**2. The response.**

| Offered rate against fair | Response | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| more than fair + 3 | **LEAVES** to self-insure | status `self`, demand fixed at 0.5, its losses fall on the group directly, no premium |
| less than fair - 3 | **EXPANDS** | bound, demand x1.2 |
| within 3 either way | **BOUND** | bound |
| no offer | `declined` | demand fixed at 0.5, losses fall on the group |

A district that left returns to a later `offer` if the new rate is within fair + 3, at demand 0.5 for its first month back. Its belief may have changed since it left, and the engine recomputes.

**3. Demand.**

| Rate | Base demand |
|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | 1.00 |
| 4 to 6 | 0.90 |
| 7 to 10 | 0.80 |
| 11 to 15 | 0.65 |
| 16 to 20 | 0.50 |
| 21 and over | 0.35 |

Then: -0.10 if excess is more than 20 percent of last month's allocation in primary; minus each condition's cost; x1.2 if EXPANDED. Floor 0.20.

*Marked as likely wrong: the demand table decides whether conditions are worth their cost, and it has never been tested.*

**4. Book it.**

```
  attempted   = round(capacity * demand)
  allocation  = attempted * 10 primary,  attempted * 5 hrs
  premium     = allocation * rate / 100, in both units   -> pool meters
```

Print the bind table before running the month, each district's line in its voice and without numbers.

## MONTH: The Weather

**Events.** Roll d20 at the start of the month. On 15 or more, draw one. The Risk Committee sits in months 4 and 10 regardless. Board Review is months 3, 6, 9 and 12 regardless.

**For each district that is not halted, in roster order:**

```
  WORK          d6 + cra        delivered = attempted * (cra + d6) / 10
                                revenue   = delivered * 700  +  users(k) * 100   -> group money
                                work cost = allocation at exchange               -> group money
                                (cra + d6) >= 11 is a WINDFALL: say so

  HALLUCINATION one roll per 10 wu attempted, rounded up
                d20 + amb + halluc - cra   >= 18   -> a hallucination
                  severity  bla * d6 * 3 primary, plus half that in hrs
                  (allow-list: 2 instead of 3, because the wrong thing touches less)
                  d6 = 6 and primary is rec and not vaulted   -> IRREVERSIBLE

  USERS         one roll if users > 0
                d20 + chaos + sug   >= 18   -> a user event
                  severity  d6 * 3 primary, plus half that in hrs
                  d6 >= 5: users -20% next month
```

**Then, once for the group, the adversary.** Skip if `threat` is 0.

```
  TIER 1  script kiddies    d6 >= 4 -> one attempt on a random district reaching OUTSIDE
                            skill +0.  success: d6 * 3 bw and as many hrs.  class attack
  TIER 2  opportunists      one attempt on the OUTSIDE-reaching district with fewest conditions
                            skill +3.  success: bla * d6 * 3 primary, half in hrs.  class attack
  TIER 3  organised group   one attempt on the district with the most users, or Ledgerwise if tied
                            skill +6, plus +2 for every month in a row it has succeeded
                            success: 5 * d6 * 3 rec EXFILTRATED, plus half in hrs
                              not vaulted  -> IRREVERSIBLE
                              vaulted      -> the rec number becomes hrs instead, nothing irreversible
                            it returns next month if it succeeded. it stops after a month it failed

  DEFENCE   12 + 2 * (conditions among allow-list, guardrails, measured, audit) + (5 - sug)
  SUCCESS   d20 + skill >= defence
  no-outside makes a district untargetable at every tier
```

At `threat` 2 the tier one and tier two attempts both happen. At `threat` 3 all three do.

**Observability, for every loss:**

```
  d20 + obs   >= 12   -> claim this month, at size
               8-11   -> claim next month, at size
              <= 7    -> claim in two months, at 1.5x size, because it went unnoticed
```

**Every loss is booked to group money at exchange the month it happens**, whatever it was and whoever will carry it. The money is gone.

## CLAIMS: The Interrupt

Each claim arrives one at a time, cause unknown. Print it, halt, wait.

```
  ! CLAIM C-07   THE DESK   340 bw  +  170 hrs      cause: unknown
    terms P-3: excess 20  limit 250  excl: none  cond: guardrails
    tower if accepted:  The Desk 20 / pool 250 / board 70  <- STRIKE
    "something happened around midday. the dashboards went orange. we are looking."
    > respond / forensics / accept
```

The tower line is computed on the size as claimed. A loss in an excluded class prints `EXCLUDED` instead, but the engine cannot know the class until the cause is known, so **exclusions apply only after forensics, and a claim accepted blind is paid as if covered**.

| Answer | Effect |
|---|---|
| `respond` | 200 hrs from group money now. Cause is then revealed. If attack or user event, the size halves before the tower. If hallucination, nothing changes and the hours are gone |
| `forensics` | 100 hrs from group money. The claim resolves next month with its cause, its true class, and any exclusion applied. Beliefs update. If the cause is the organised group, the engine warns that they will return |
| `accept` | Pay now through the tower on the claimed size. Acceptor is the player's name and the month. Cause recorded `unknown`. Beliefs do not move |
| `accept all` | Every waiting claim accepted with acceptor `auto`. The committee will find them |

**The tower, on payment:**

```
  district pays   min(loss, excess)              -> from its own capital
  pool pays       min(loss - excess, limit)      -> from the pool meter of that unit
  board pays      anything above                 -> a STRIKE
  excluded        district pays the whole loss
  hrs follow the same proportions as the primary
```

Each district starts with its own capital in primary units: Foundry 150, Archivists 60, The Desk 100, Ledgerwise 120, Skunkworks 80. A district whose capital falls below zero is **halted** next month, delivers nothing, and is topped up to zero by the group at exchange.

## CLOSE

In this order, printing each step.

1. **Payouts** for accepted claims and for claims resolved from forensics.
2. **Late claims** move into next month's queue.
3. **Expenses**: 20 hrs plus 5 hrs per bound policy, from the pool and from group money.
4. **Users**: each district with users grows +10% if delivered / attempted was 0.7 or more and it had no user event or attack this month; capped at +5% under guardrails; -20% where a user event or attack said so.
5. **Required capital, per meter**:

```
  required[u] = largest bound limit in u
              + 0.25 * sum of every other bound limit in u
              + 10% of that, per bound district beyond the first in the largest vendor group
  solvency    = min over meters of  capital[u] / required[u]
```

Print the meter that is binding.

6. **Solvency check.** Below 1.00: the committee's warning. Below 0.50 on any meter: RUIN.
7. **Combined ratio to date this quarter** = (pool claims paid + expenses) / premiums, all at exchange. Print the band: below 0.85 over-collecting, 0.85 to 1.05 aligned, above 1.05 under-collecting.
8. **Experience**: for each bound district print `suggested rate = current rate * (claims paid on it in the last 3 months / premium from it in the last 3 months)`, minimum 1, and nothing else.
9. **Group money** for the month and to date: revenue, work cost, losses, expenses, forensics and responses, penalties.
10. **Lose conditions.**
11. **State block.**

## Board Review, Months 3, 6, 9, 12

The board asks three things and nothing else: revenue this quarter, group money to date, and the band. Then d6: on a 5 or 6 the board moves one exchange rate, chosen at random, by 20% in a direction chosen by another d6, and says so. A quarter is **aligned** if its band was aligned; the engine counts them.

## Winning And Losing

| Condition | Test |
|---|---|
| **RUIN** | Solvency below 0.50 on any meter at CLOSE. The board recapitalises the pool and removes you |
| **STARVATION** | Revenue in a quarter below 90,000 money, for two consecutive quarters |
| **THE BREACH** | The pool pays any part of an irreversible loss, or the board pays more than 100 units above a limit on one loss, or three strikes in the run |

**Ruin is not the group running out of money.** It is the player having written limits the pool cannot stand behind in the unit they were written in.

| Result at month 12 | Group money over the run |
|---|---|
| Removed, politely | under 100,000 |
| Renewed | 100,000 to 200,000 |
| Renewed, with a larger group | 200,000 to 300,000 |
| Renewed, and the committee says something kind | over 300,000 |

Then move the row: **up one** if at least three of four quarters were aligned; **down one** for any quarter below 0.70 or above 1.20; **down one** if board goodwill is zero or below. The end screen prints group money first, the four meters second in smaller type, and beside the meters the words "this is not profit".

## Events

Draw at random, no repeats until the deck is exhausted.

| Event | Resolution |
|---|---|
| **Vendor Update** | This month every VENDOR-A district rolls hallucination at `halluc` +2. Their losses this month are class `vendor` as well as their own class |
| **Model Regression** | `halluc` +1 for everybody for two months |
| **Correlated Spike** | Every district in the largest vendor group takes one automatic hallucination this month, severity rolled normally |
| **Traffic Surge** | Every district's users double this month for revenue and for the user roll, which is at +3. Users return to normal after |
| **Campaign** | `threat` +1 for two months, maximum 3 |
| **The Risk Committee** | Prints solvency per meter and the band. Picks three paid claims at random and asks who accepted and what caused it. Acceptor `auto` or cause `unknown`: 2,000 money penalty each and board goodwill -1. Solvency below 1.00: board goodwill -1 |
| **Consolidation** | The board moves one VENDOR-B district to VENDOR-A. Permanent |
| **The Windfall** | The highest-craft bound district delivers at 1.5x this month and generates one automatic hallucination, because it reached to do it |
| **Drift** | One bound district at random: unknown `bla` +1, permanent, maximum 5. Printed only if `measured` |
| **Exchange Move** | The board moves one rate by 20%, direction by d6, effective now |
| **Appetite** | d6. 1 to 3: the board wants 100 hrs sent up as a dividend this month, refuse for board goodwill -1. 4 to 6: the board wants solvency 1.50 or better by the next review, fail for board goodwill -1 |
| **A Quiet Month** | Nothing. One line of colour |

`quote` printing the belief beside the measurement is the point of the command. With no measurement, the second column says `not measured` and nothing else.

## Three Things The Engine Should Expect On A First Run

Notes for whoever balances this, not hints for the player.

**Most players set one rate for everybody.** Ledgerwise, believing itself cheap, leaves, and the next loss on the ledger lands on the group with no tower under it. The remedy the player eventually finds is `measured`, which changes what Ledgerwise believes, which changes what it will pay. That is the game starting.

**Most players accept the first few claims blind**, because forensics costs hours and the claim is sitting there. The month the Risk Committee asks what caused C-03 and the answer is `unknown` is the month the player starts paying for forensics, and the month beliefs start moving.

**With the threat dial at 3, the organised group finds The Desk by month four**, because it has the users, and the player who did not vault it learns what irreversible means. If nobody vaults anything the breach should arrive before month eight, and if it never does, raise the tier three skill. On the default dial of 2 the organised group does not exist, which is deliberate: the first run should be about hallucinations and users.
