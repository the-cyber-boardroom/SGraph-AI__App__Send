# Changelog — App-iframe capabilities Phase 3: HUD privileges chip

**Version:** v0.27.79 · **Date:** 2026-05-27 · **Author:** Dev (branch `claude/exciting-brown-G2P9Z`)

## Summary

Visibility only (no consent yet): the HUD now shows what the app is allowed to do, making the
otherwise-invisible `app.json` grants legible to the user.

- `app-hud.setPrivileges(perm)` renders a `🔓 write · move · …` chip (sibling of the read-only badge);
  hidden when the app has only default reads.
- `app-shell` includes `perm` in the `app-shell:ready` detail; `en-gb/app/index.html` calls
  `hud.setPrivileges(e.detail.perm)` alongside `setInfo`.

## Test impact
- DOM-only; no jsdom-free unit. Syntax-clean. Manual: open apps with various permission blocks and
  confirm the chip text; read-only/no-grant apps show no chip.
