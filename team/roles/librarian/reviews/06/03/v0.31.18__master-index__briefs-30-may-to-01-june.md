## Thematic Grouping

### Theme A: Artefact Layer (30 May)
Documents 2–4 form a coherent "artefact production" layer: vault-per-standard turns compliance documents into semantic graph artefacts (P-281); public-preview-with-read-only-key makes them shareable with one link (P-282); the vulnerability-threat-intel vault is the first capstone demo combining both (P-283). All three depend on existing VIV + public previews + read-only tokens infrastructure.

### Theme B: Content & Publishing Engine (30 May – 31 May)
Documents 5, 7, 8, 9 form the "website as living knowledge base" arc: the library becomes the shop front (P-284) → the site is controlled by agents via per-section vaults and a vault CI pipeline (P-285) → industry-specific pages embed real vault examples (P-286) → every page serves its semantic graph for machines (P-287).

### Theme C: Partner Ecosystem (1 June)
Documents 11–17 map SG/Vault onto a seven-layer partner stack. Netlify (hosting, P-288) + Daytona (execution, P-289) + Convex (live backend, P-290) + Pi (harness, P-291) + HeyGen (video consumption, P-292) + Semantic skills graphs (foundation, P-294) + Tavon (channel, P-293). Partners converge on the same Skills/MCP/provenance primitives SG/Vault has bet on — a strong external validation signal.

### Theme D: Skills Creator Economy (1 June)
Documents 18–22 build a vertically integrated skills economy on top of the partner layer: base vaults (P-295) → marketplace with trustworthy scoring (P-296) → opinionated expertise as the commercial model (P-297) → comparison vault + CLI-wrapping + token optimisation (P-298) → OpenRouter broker as billing substrate (P-299).
