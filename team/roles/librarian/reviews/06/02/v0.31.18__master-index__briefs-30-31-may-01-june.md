### Theme B: Publishing and Content Strategy (05/30–05/31)
The library becomes the shop front with default-to-publish (P-283). Every vault, example, and skill flows there. The multi-agent content workflow (content vault + comms vault + FS email) is claimed as working. Industry-by-industry use-case pages (P-287) are the outreach layer driven by the core metrics (vaults created/accessed, files loaded).

### Theme C: Agent-Controlled Web Infrastructure (05/31)
Agent-controlled websites (P-285) + per-page semantic graphs (P-286) form a coherent web infrastructure stack: agents author in vaults, sections render from vaults via public read keys, per-page semantic graphs and llms.txt serve LLMs and crawlers, and the vault CI pipeline (dev → QA → prod vault promotion) governs releases.

Seven partners mapped to seven stack layers:
- **Hosting:** Netlify AX (P-288)
- **Execution:** Daytona (P-289)
- **Live backend:** Convex (P-290)

- **Channel:** Tavon (P-293)
- **Foundation:** Semantic graphs for skills (P-294)

Most partners converge on Skills, MCP, and provenance — validating SG/Vault's bets.

### Theme E: Skills Creator Economy (06/01)
