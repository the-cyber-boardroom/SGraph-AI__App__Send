### Feature Stream 3: Infrastructure Fix (05/08)

| Commit | Time | Change | Impact |
|--------|------|--------|--------|
| `b61a181` | 08 May 23:58 | `Storage_FS__S3` boto3 client now lazy (SnapStart fix) | Production reliability |

**Root cause resolved:** `setup()` called `bucket_exists()` at Lambda init → boto3 client
serialised into SnapStart snapshot including urllib3 connection pool → stale TCP connections
after restore → intermittent Lambda timeouts. Fix: `setup()` returns `self` immediately;
`_s3()` creates client lazily on first actual request.
