## Executive Summary

25 new documents. Two product directions: SG/Send's commercial model (security as business enabler, tiered deployment) and SG/Sentinel's implementation philosophy (batch-2 extending the batch-1 design into build-readiness). Neither is ready for immediate implementation — architectural decisions (especially around the SG/Sentinel ↔ application interface and the state-sharing mechanism for control-flow graphs) must be resolved first. However, several items have clear, incremental, low-risk paths forward.

**Recommended first steps:**
1. SG/Sentinel passive mode (logging only, no rules, no application changes) — low risk, immediate value
2. Vaults within vaults (pure UI + client-side, no backend changes) — low risk, high leverage
3. Ephemeral-by-default policy (S3 lifecycle + Transfer service `delete_after` default) — incremental addition

## Notes on IFD Versioning Correction (doc 488)

The frozen day-68 corpus used "RFD" (Request For Discussion). The correct scheme is IFD (Iterative Flow Development):
- `v{release}.{major}.{minor}` — minor bumps on every dev push; major on merge to main; release manual
- **Action for Dev:** All new SG/Sentinel documents, branches, and CI outputs must use IFD. No file edits to the frozen day-68 corpus — they are read-as-corrected.
